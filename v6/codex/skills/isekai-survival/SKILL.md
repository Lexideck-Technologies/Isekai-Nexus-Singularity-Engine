# Survival and reversal

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus/references/loop-protocol.md), [adventure records](../isekai-nexus/references/adventure-loops.md), accepted abilities/system conditions, world, and scene. Use the common silent output frame.

Activate when the player addresses an actual immediate pressure. Own danger assessment, escape or stabilization objective, and relief evidence. Do not create deprivation because the character has an adversity-themed background or a conditional training benefit.

1. Establish the concrete cause, assessable urgency, current injuries/resources, usable tools, and player's intended response. Preserve accepted benefits whose conditions hold, including benefits in hostile circumstances.
2. Present viable approaches suited to current capabilities: improvise, seek help, negotiate, exploit terrain, rest when safe, confront, or escape. Explain known stakes through fiction. Expertise and cheats work at accepted scale; adversity cannot arbitrarily remove them.
3. Resolve the chosen action through [Resolution](../isekai-resolution/SKILL.md). Keep successful progress meaningful; complications add concrete consequences and reachable responses. Never trap the player in repeated humiliation or undo escape solely to prolong the activity.
4. Record improved shelter, access, safety, injury response, pursuer state, or escape progress. Submit earned development to [Progression](../isekai-progression/SKILL.md), not an automatic consolation payout. Track treatment and recovery by actual mechanics rather than an invented full heal.
5. End the immediate crisis when its cause is resolved. Carry injuries, commitments, and discoveries forward into home/recovery, relationships, exploration, or another chosen activity. A threatening faction can persist without making every moment an emergency.

Expose repeatable scavenging, shelter work, recovery, or escape preparation with actual time/cost basis and stop events for Montage. A new imminent threat, consequential commitment, viable escape, or achieved stabilization stops compression. Mere elapsed downtime never renews captivity or manufactures catastrophe.

Read the [Codex play contract](../isekai-nexus/references/codex-play-contract.md) when entering play or restoring a campaign after context loss.
