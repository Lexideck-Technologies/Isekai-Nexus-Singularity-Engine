# Home and Community

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus/references/loop-protocol.md), [play map](../isekai-nexus/references/play-loops.md), [home and community rules](../isekai-nexus/references/social-rules.md), accepted character, resolved world, and scene state. Read [economy rules](../isekai-nexus/references/economy-rules.md) for actual production/upkeep.

## Entry and ownership

Activate for the player's declared activity in this cover after arrival, or resume an interrupted objective. A start, quoted instruction, or administrative question does not activate it. Own the local objective, stage and evidence; use the shared owners for causal results, advancement, inventory, relationships and territory. Never regrant starting assets. Use the authoritative adapted helper tables and rules.

Keep one local record: objective, stage, participants, evidence, resources/commitments, pending choice, and connected objectives. Recurring stages: chosen need -> activity -> meaningful improvement -> new choice. Change activity when the player chooses without erasing this record.

## Procedure

1. Read the player's chosen household or community goal and preferred pace. Quiet play is complete gameplay; do not replace it with a mandatory battle, catastrophe, or distant-threat countdown.
2. Establish home access, neighbors, available tools/food/materials, actual upkeep, commitments, and desired result. A room or garden does not require domain unlock. Follow Domain only when territorial or organizational authority is actually involved.
3. Resolve the declared action through Shared Resolution. Route recipes, expenditure, stock and trade to Craft and Trade; route neighbor/companion responses to Relationships. Share one attempt for a lesson, cultivated garden, or prepared meal across every cover.
4. Record real improvement, practiced technique, discovered fact, care, or relationship response. Submit demonstrated challenge/mastery to Progression. Routine passage of time never grants automatic EXP; a meaningful quiet accomplishment can justify advancement under its actual rule.
5. Establish sustainable repetition only after tools, quantities, time, and commitments are known. Keep actual upkeep visible when useful. Do not invent property title, servants, debt, or household income.
6. Preserve unfinished projects across departure or interruption. Present a natural next choice, peaceful satisfaction, visitor, or practical opportunity; no danger is required to finish the response.
7. Render one scene with consequence, relevant dialogue or information, any actual change, and a reachable next choice. Use the loop protocol frame without visible section labels. Stop at unresolved player agency. Answer administrative questions without advancing time.

## Compression attachment

Expose the repeatable task, current stage, evidence requirement, quantities/resources, time per task or accepted period basis, and stop events. Stop repetition when input limits end, capability is uncertain, a new agreement is needed, or a consequential player choice appears. Send each shared outcome to its owner once. The Montage procedure supplies the interval and pacing, not a new reward schedule.

## Output

```text
{World_Response_To_Chosen_Activity}
{Relevant_Dialogue_Or_Practical_Result}
{Actual_Changes_If_Any}

{Reachable_Continuation_Or_Material_Choice}
```

Omit empty elements. Never print skill names, source node addresses, routing, internal state keys, or rule headings in the player's scene.
