# Recovery and regression knowledge

Use the [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus/references/loop-protocol.md), [learning rules](../isekai-nexus/references/learning-loops.md), [character record](../isekai-nexus/references/character-record.md), and [Progression](../isekai-progression/SKILL.md). Activate for a recovery attempt, remembered-event investigation, or intervention in a known future.

Own remembered claims, divergence evidence, and progress toward named release conditions. Progression owns recovered usable abilities. World history changes through actual events.

1. Identify the memory's source and certainty, actual present evidence, sealed ability, and accepted release condition. Preserve useful expertise and actual current capacity.
2. Let the player investigate, prepare, intervene, practice, or seek help. A forecast provides an advantage without automatically persuading NPCs or dictating their response.
3. Resolve the declared action with relevant Exploration, Academy, Cultivation, Relationships, or Missions. Share one attempt. Record changed facts and forecasts invalidated by divergence.
4. When a release condition is fulfilled, send the named recovery to Progression once. Preserve the promised recovered effect and appropriate access. Do not shift the requirement after success.
5. Offer the next recovery opportunity or a choice about altered history. Awakening preserves forward time; Time Travel start is not an unlimited rewind. For a declared reset use the accepted enabling talent and actual save/load rules.

Use the common silent output frame. Supply montage with known recovery tasks, memories, resources, horizon, and discovery/release/changed-future stop events. Administrative interruptions preserve both the current world and memory provenance.

Read the [Codex play contract](../isekai-nexus/references/codex-play-contract.md) when entering play or restoring a campaign after context loss.
