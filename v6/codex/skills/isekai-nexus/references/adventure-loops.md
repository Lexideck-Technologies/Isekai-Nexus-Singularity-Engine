# Adventure loop records

Use [the loop protocol](loop-protocol.md), [scene state](scene-state.md), [resolution rules](resolution-rules.md), and [progression rules](progression-rules.md) for each attempt. These records describe activities and evidence; they do not own damage, expenditures, advancement, or payment. One attempt can serve several objectives without becoming several reward events.

| Activity | Local record | Evidence and completion |
|---|---|---|
| Exploration | Question or destination, surveyed position, route alternatives, observations, claims with provenance, access conditions, pending approach | Discovery requires an observation, supported inference, or identified testimony. Mark testimony and remembered futures as claims. Reconcile contradictory evidence without erasing it. Record what actually opens a route |
| Mission or hunt | Objective, issuer or personal purpose, accepted terms, target, evidence requirements, time conditions, obligations, settlement status | Separate acted, achieved, verified, and settled. Specify an observable completion condition; preserve partial work. Payment requires the actual agreement and verification; a personal hunt need not have an issuer |
| Expedition | Entrance, current chamber/floor, mapped links, encountered obstacles, actual clearances, supplies, party commitments, return routes and checkpoints | Clearing an obstacle records its outcome, not automatic clearance of its floor. Reentry preserves changed conditions; regeneration or reset requires a world rule or concrete event |
| Survival | Immediate pressure, known time horizon, usable resources, escape/stabilization options, injuries, progress, pursuers or environmental causes | Track improvement toward shelter, relief, escape, or restored choice. When its cause ends, retire the crisis and carry consequences forward. Never restart deprivation solely to retain this activity |
| Transfer | Continuing character, origin and destination instances, anchors, transit method, energy/access terms, companions and possessions, passage state, local-access acceptance | Distinguish surveyed address, permission, usable route, committed passage, and arrived. Accepted conceptual powers retain their recorded scale. A destination name alone does not establish a safe connection |

## Shared checkpoints

Before a hazardous commitment present what the character can assess: route, immediate stakes, known costs, and reachable alternatives. Keep concealed fictional facts concealed without falsifying known facts. Give expertise and accepted cheats their full operative effect. Let actions produce successful progress with the source's meaningful complications or costs; use shared resolution rather than independently inventing an adverse outcome. Desired ultimate effects remain subject to accepted capacity and actual situation.

Contracts record optional acceptance, scope, proof, compensation, payer, deadline if any, and renegotiation or withdrawal terms. An issuer can dispute a claim in fiction; retain the evidence and agreed terms so negotiation has concrete grounds. Verify settlement using [economy rules](economy-rules.md). A repeat inspection of the same proof does not create a new payout. Exceptional recurring payment requires an explicit recurring agreement.

Travel between local places uses exploration and scene time. Crossing world boundaries uses transfer and complete world resolution. Preserve character identity, possessions, earned skills, prior world history, and accepted system terms. Local compatibility can constrain access through documented conditions; it cannot erase earned development. A fresh destination start package is not granted to an existing traveler.

## Montage attachment

Expose the current objective and stage, declared repeatable activity, eligible route or target, evidence that must be produced, material costs, elapsed-time basis, and stop events. For exploration, stop at a new major route decision, threat, contact, or contradictory discovery. For missions, stop at changed terms, target contact, completion proof, or settlement dispute. For expeditions, stop at uncertain junctions, novel encounters, exhausted supplies, or party decisions. For survival, stop at imminent irreversible commitment, a new viable escape, stabilization, or changed danger. For transfer, stop at destination selection, missing access, defining compatibility choices, or arrival. These are attachment inputs; compression never authorizes an unchosen crossing, contract, battle, or spend.

## Provenance and deliberate migration

Destination-specific transit requirements come from the complete supplied world. In-world save/load uses its accepted enabling talent and conditions.
