# Montage agreement

Montage compresses presentation and advances declared activities through their existing procedures. It targets one loop or several cooperating loops. It has no independent EXP, reward, success, failure, cultivation, or relationship formula.

## Interval record

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Identity and incoming state | Interval identity, character/world revision, starting location/time, existing effects |
| Scope | Chosen objectives, participating loops, repeatable activities, desired horizon or stop condition |
| Delegation | Routine actions authorized, acceptable approaches, decisions reserved for the player |
| Budget | Available and permitted materials, funds, effort, travel, recovery, other actual commitments |
| Schedule | Sequential or concurrent activities, time basis, existing appointments/deadlines |
| Evidence | Distinct challenges, demonstrated changes, task/contract proofs, actual completed work |
| Checkpoints | Committed attempts/effects, elapsed time, remaining plan, earliest stop event |
| Return | Current scene and pending choice; completed, paused, revised, or abandoned interval |

Capture supplied facts and infer ordinary scheduling from the accepted plan. Ask only a material missing choice. “Study for a week” can reuse the current lesson and method; it does not authorize spending all savings or accepting a new pact. An unspecified horizon needs a bounded proposal or one question before skipping time. “Until I master this” permits progress toward the stated goal, not a promise that any goal is reached in an invented duration.

## Scheduling and causal order

Use established task durations and recipe yields. If none exists, establish a world-compatible duration/quantity for this plan and distinguish it from a source formula. Qualitative durations are valid. Resolve sequential activities from each preceding result; do not resolve a month's work against an unchanged opening record. Concurrent objectives can share an activity, but active time and resources cannot be used twice. Include actual recovery, attendance requirements, travel, supply access, and relevant obligations. Optional patron tasks remain optional.

Resolve representative meaningful attempts and genuine repeated batches. Group already-established routine work into a batch with its actual quantity, resource consumption, time, and evidence. Do not fabricate a successful challenge for every repeated motion or compute EXP as days multiplied by a guessed rate. Select observable milestones rather than narrating every exercise. Accepted accelerated or unlimited-benefit systems keep their actual promise; no new cap is imposed merely to limit compressed advancement.

Check established world events during elapsed time. NPCs and institutions continue according to their actual circumstances. No automatic invasion, relationship decay, catastrophe, Crown Lock failure, or tribulation arises merely because the player chose downtime. A genuinely scheduled event remains relevant. Preserve remembered-history claims and actual divergence.

## Effect and checkpoint agreement

Use [scene state](scene-state.md) and [loop protocol](loop-protocol.md). Resolution adjudicates all results and costs and commits health/MP/conditions/time. Economy commits inventory/funds. Progression commits earned advancement. Social and Domain commit their respective facts. Montage owns the interval, schedule, pacing, checkpoints, and return; it commits none of those effects a second time.

One batch has one attempt identity and separately owned effect identities. A garden practical can support academy, cultivation, and technique development through one outcome; it earns one EXP award plus any distinct justified control/technique effects. A verified recurring contract can pay for genuinely separate work; repeating its receipt cannot pay again. Domain income uses actual eligible periods, never one payment per narrated scene.

Apply milestones in order for every earned level. Leave unspent points and pending evolution choices intact unless the player already delegated a valid spending rule. Pause at a material evolution or power change needing acceptance. A passive increase with no reserved choice can occur during compression. Save checkpoints in available state before narration; durable writes require actual storage. If interrupted after partial application, retain applied identities and finish only pending owner handoffs. Do not rewind an already elapsed interval because narration was interrupted.

## Stop events

Stop at the earliest event requiring a choice outside the declared delegation:

- Budget, supplies, health, or resource reserve reaches its stated limit.
- A new bargain, binding commitment, risky method, material purchase, or important relationship decision appears.
- A breakthrough, class replacement, release condition, or Authority discovery needs the player's direction.
- A novel threat, uncertain route, meaningful mission encounter, disputed settlement, or changed future requires a new approach.
- A scheduled event changes what can reasonably continue.
- An unchosen world passage is ready to commit, or a new arrival scene begins. An explicitly delegated, valid crossing uses Travel and Arrival; the tailored introduction returns to full scene pacing.

Do not freeze routine work at every small variation. Honor explicitly delegated low-risk decisions and previously accepted methods. A declared hunt can compress established routine encounters within its agreed scope; a new materially different threat returns to a scene. Summarize only completed activity, state the actual elapsed period and progress in-world, then present the live choice. Preserve the unspent budget and unfinished plan.

## Render and resume

Use a few concrete moments tied to the character's attention, methods, relationships, and world. Show change through practiced ease, an improved design, a tested technique, useful information, or actual earned recognition. Include concise meaningful numbers when established and useful; keep internal ledgers and category labels silent. Do not claim success for a pending attempt or an accepted relationship for an unasked NPC.

Resume the remaining interval from its checkpoint after the player resolves the stop event or changes the plan. A new duration extends the plan from current time; it does not rerun the completed interval. Status and administrative questions consume no fictional time. Revoking compression returns to the current full scene with actual progress preserved.

Montage applies the shared mechanics to the player-approved plan.
