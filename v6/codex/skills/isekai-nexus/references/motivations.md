# Guiding motivations

| Choice | Desire | Priorities for character evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Control one's fate after helplessness | Combat, command, high-impact abilities |
| Freedom | Escape crushing responsibilities or debts | Self-sufficiency, stealth, versatile abilities |
| Understanding | Solve the unsolvable or learn the forbidden | Knowledge, analysis, magic |
| Redemption | Atone for failure or wrongdoing | Protection, creation, community building |
| Peace | A slow life after chaos | Defense, utility, noncombat development |
| Love | Connection after loneliness | Relationships, social standing, faction intrigue; offer the court overlay for chosen social play |

Use the player's chosen meaning. Preserve a custom motive in their words. Treat an inferred motive as a proposal until accepted. These priorities guide later evaluation; ingestion awards no stats or abilities.
