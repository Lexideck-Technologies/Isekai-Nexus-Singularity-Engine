# Domain rules

Authority: preserved core DOMAIN.UNLOCK, TERRITORY.TIERS, DOMAIN.DEVELOP, DOMAIN.CHALLENGES. Authoritative tables follow. Ordinary household life and helping neighbors require no domain unlock. A domain means actual territorial or organizational authority.

## Gates and state

Either Level 25+ OR a completed major quest granting land/title/organizational leadership unlocks the domain layer. Allied/Revered faction standing supports a qualifying quest; it never replaces either gate. Eligibility does not grant land, followers, or revenue. Establish actual possession/recognized leadership and commitments. Record unlock proof, authority basis, tier, population, resources, income, costs, projects, delegated roles, treaties, challenges, and accounting periods.

## Development and consequences

Territory income uses Silver at F-D and Gold at C-AA under the shared currency conversion. AA legendary artifacts remain AA+ loot and crafting capacity; owning a kingdom does not automatically generate an artifact.

Choose an objective and focus. Check its listed skill/talent/knowledge requirement; establish labor, financing, materials, approvals, time, and completion evidence. Shared Resolution adjudicates causal attempts and all cost proposals, committing only health, MP, physical conditions, and elapsed time. Domain owns projects, authority commitments, territory state, tier eligibility, and accounting-period evidence. Economy commits each inventory/funds movement from the same proposals once. Economy applies receipts/payments once; Social owns faction/individual changes; Progression owns earned abilities/titles.

Tier income bands are monthly reference income, not immediate rewards or guaranteed net profit. Record actual income and basis; account for actual upkeep/disruption. Source gives no universal taxation/expense formula. Apply income once per completed month or a previously accepted proration convention. Overlapping scenes and montage cannot collect it repeatedly. Population and functioning capabilities support tier advancement; personal Level 25 never turns an outpost into a kingdom. Source tiers end at AA; higher tiers need accepted world rules.

Challenges arise from actual resource balance, rivals, threats, or delegation. Never impose invasion/betrayal solely because time passes. Distant threats keep their actual timeline; ordinary downtime never causes automatic doom. Establish succession/delegated authority where relevant. Sovereignty/Dominion authorities use accepted conceptual terms and domain scaling; political rank grants no missing conceptual power.
