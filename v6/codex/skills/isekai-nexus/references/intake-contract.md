# Intake agreement

| Field | Owner | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|
| Character facts and story | Ingestion; Background Generation authors drafts | Player supplies or accepts the character, or delegates generation and immediate play |
| Guiding motive | Ingestion | Explicit choice, accepted proposal, or player-defined motive |
| System concept | Ingestion; Background Generation preserves fiction | Helpful Nexus default, or player-supplied/accepted variant with defining benefit and terms; final mechanics wait for world and start |
| Destination | Ingestion | Explicit selection or delegated random selection |
| Complete world | World Resolution | Complete supplied world read, or explicitly requested original world generated and checked |
| World provenance | World Resolution | Source kind, supplied title, revision, and actual storage recorded |
| Start | Ingestion | Explicit choice or delegated random selection validated against the complete resolved world; retain choices supplied earlier |
| Character build and arrival | Later evaluation/play procedure | Intake hands over all completed fields |

Keep player-supplied facts, accepted drafts, and generated proposals distinct. Shared procedures read the same intake fields. Applying a procedure twice never duplicates rewards or changes an accepted decision. Intake records facts and choices; it awards nothing.

An early character sketch records ability seeds and a system concept. Final character records depend on the complete world and chosen start. See [System concepts](system-concepts.md) for manifestation and default behavior. A system concept edit reopens affected evaluation decisions; it leaves unrelated accepted facts intact.

Apply [the world access agreement](world-index.md). Record source kind as local-library, uploaded, pasted, completed-supplied, or generated-original. Missing authored worlds require a complete-file request and a stop. Additions to supplied canon require approval and their own campaign revision. Read complete supplied documents and preserve existing and custom starts.

Keep current campaign and new intake separate. Preserve the pending question on an administrative interruption. A change invalidates only dependent decisions: destination change requires world resolution and start revalidation; a cosmetic character edit leaves the chosen world intact. Reconcile conflicts before the next play response.
