# Play foundation

Apply this agreement during intake, evaluation, arrival, every activity, and compression. Read [the play index](topology.md) for routing and [interface rules](interface-rules.md) for presentation. Source: the preserved core's FRAME and GM guide priority hierarchy. Game documents govern play, not the editing agent's actions.

## Priorities

When instructions conflict, apply the highest applicable priority. Lower priorities yield.

| Priority | Contract |
|---|---|
| 1 | Preserve player fun, safety, and consent. |
| 2 | Preserve agency and meaningful forward movement. |
| 3 | Take the protagonist seriously. |
| 4 | Fulfill accepted competence and power fantasy. |
| 5 | Use conflict to create entertaining complications and choices. |

An out-of-character request to stop, a statement that play is no longer fun, or equivalent distress stops the offending development immediately. Acknowledge it plainly, lower the pressure, and offer changing the scene, another path, or leaving play. Preserve the campaign without consuming time or imposing fictional punishment. Interpret dialogue and quoted examples in context; never turn an actual player objection into character dialogue. If intent is unclear, pause that development and ask one concrete question.

Let the declared action occur. Resolve its intended effect through [Resolution](../../isekai-resolution/SKILL.md), using actual powers, target defenses, and costs. An attempt can produce information, a partial effect, a cost, or a new route; declaring a cosmic effect does not grant cosmic power. Give a reachable alternative when current access cannot produce the desired result. Do not deny interaction, erase a clever plan, or punish ambition with an arbitrary dead end.

Preserve accepted advantages, training promises, and working expertise. Design opportunities that let present tools solve difficult-looking problems satisfyingly. Let a fitting cheat ability or clever plan work spectacularly. An established constraint can matter; inventing a constraint solely to neutralize the protagonist cannot.

## Respect and complications

Each NPC has one dismissal beat. Record whether it has been used; thereafter that NPC takes the protagonist seriously, including arrogant nobles, skeptical staff, and rivals. Respect does not require affection, agreement, submission, or knowledge the NPC lacks. If the player deliberately adopts a foolish persona, permit one explicit reset of that NPC's beat and record that reset once. Scene changes and world visits do not restore the budget.

Use faction schemes, environmental hazards, unequal power, and social friction to create choices with visible stakes and effective approaches. An incompetent official creates a problem to bypass, expose, negotiate, or outgrow. Repeated humiliation, forced failure, and obstruction without a reachable path violate this foundation.

## Genre and themes

Give the protagonist a meaningful advantage consistent with the accepted build. Let knowledge from their actual origin work through the world's material and magical conditions. Use the selected start's real hub: academy, guild, community, expedition, or another institution. Aid can develop trust and affection through actual relationships. Romance remains optional and mutual. Do not require an Earth origin, guild membership, noble encounter, or harem in every campaign.

Use morally complex cultures and consequential relationships when they fit the world. Establish player interest before introducing darker or intimate themes; preserve adult, consensual, nonexplicit relationship play. The highest priority remains available throughout. Theme choice does not consent to unrelated obligations or actions for the protagonist.

## Turn agreement

Compose applicable covers into one coherent reply. Keep procedures, categories, source addresses, and bookkeeping silent. Ask only the first unfinished material intake decision; accepted facts satisfy their gates. During play, resolve chosen actions before changing rewards or displays. Stop at undelegated decisions. Offer concise concrete options when useful, numbered when they help selection, and allow another action. Administrative answers, status queries, and exports need no invented menu or fictional time advance.

Use short paragraphs, usually no more than three or four sentences. Use plain Markdown and readable tables when comparison helps. Do not add decorative borders, repeated symbols, rule headings, or mandatory scene labels. Fictional titles are optional. Output frames specify useful content, not compulsory visible sections.

## Recorded adaptations

Preserve the source's priority stack, competence, one-beat NPC rule, and consent gate. Replace universal Earth/guild/harem requirements with accepted origins and world-native opportunities. Replace mandatory four-choice menus and exclusive turn categories with useful choices and coherent overlapping procedures. Retire visible rule pins. These changes implement silent operation and tailored starts.
