# Social rules

Authority: preserved core ENTITIES, SOCIAL, and COMPANIONS. Authoritative adapted catalogues follow. Adapt names to the world; rank describes capacity and never mandates every archetype. Preserve NPC identity, goals, knowledge, commitments, and agency. Keep individual affinity, faction reputation, and public-image Reputation separate. Resolve actual interactions through Shared Resolution; record evidence for every change once. Progression owns EXP and earned abilities.

## Adjudication

Allied NPCs supply the possible companion pool; recruitment still follows its method and agreement. The S-rank Keeper of Cosmic Balance is also the granting entity for the source Chrono-Janitor rare class. That connection opens an opportunity under the class's actual gates and never awards the class for meeting the Keeper. The SSS Outer God shares the source Abstract Horror's resistance to conventional combat; route an encounter through its actual threat rules.

Elegance, Charisma, public Reputation, and social Intellect are GM-tracked and revealable through an accepted Appraisal talent. Social Intellect measures political acumen; preserve its connection to Diplomatic domain development without silently replacing the character's INT attribute.

Love motivation opens opportunities without forced romance. Activate the court overlay for chosen courtship or sustained social maneuvering. Its 3-5 cast members remain independent people; introduce them through scenes. Adapt gender and orientation to the player and world. Romance involves adults and mutual consent. Affection, rescue, rank, gifts, and contracts never substitute for consent. Platonic/familial bonds share the affinity spine. Intimate scenes focus on emotional intimacy and remain non-explicit. Establish consent before darker or intimate themes. Do not import private agent personas.

Rescue provides the source Friendly(+15) recruitment opportunity; joining requires voluntary agreement. This explicitly replaces source automatic recruitment. Loyalty descriptions express inclination, not compelled sacrifice. Magical bonds follow accepted terms. An inactive network stays available; do not erase it or degrade affinity simply because the player chooses another loop. Establish an actual neglected expectation and meaningful elapsed time before relationship decay.

The source gives faction bands and recruitment seeds but no universal reputation delta formula or numeric affinity thresholds. Preserve its qualitative relationship stages. Record a campaign convention if numerical adjudication is required; never invent canonical thresholds. NPC response follows personality, evidence, and knowledge; persuasion is not guaranteed agreement.

Choose and record an active companion cap of 3 or 4; the source leaves the choice open. The inactive network is unlimited. Divide earned base party EXP among all active members once, then apply each companion affinity rate and the protagonist Growth Boost only to the protagonist share. Progression owns award and rounding convention; source rounding is unspecified. Loot follows an actual party distribution agreement through economy. Coaching, equipment, and potions support native growth with evidence; one outcome grants no duplicate EXP.

## Companion Limit

Active party: record a cap of 3 or 4 companions for narrative focus. Inactive network: unlimited, available for specific tasks. Maintaining high affinity involves meaningful attention, gifts, and shared experiences. Decay requires actual neglected expectations and meaningful elapsed time.

## Party Rules

Divide base EXP among all active members. Apply Growth Boost only to the protagonist share; apply companion affinity rates to their shares through Progression. Party leadership distributes loot under the recorded agreement. Allies usually cooperate; neutral mercenaries can refuse dangerous orders without additional pay. Followers grow at native rates; coaching, high-quality loot, and rare potions can accelerate growth with evidence.
