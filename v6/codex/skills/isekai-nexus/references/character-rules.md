# Character rules

These tables govern character evaluation and later progression together. Apply the recorded world, start, and player-approved concept together. Do not turn a catalogue into a player shopping list.

## Power and expertise

Source: `POWERTIER.TABLE`.

| Rank | Character level or state | Capacity |
|---|---|---|
| F | 1-5 | Untrained populace |
| D | 6-15 | Novice, trained soldier or rookie adventurer |
| C | 16-30 | Veteran professional |
| B | 31-50 | Elite, town hero or knightly captain |
| A | 51-70 | Master, kingdom champion |
| AA | 71-90 | National threat, archmage or sword saint |
| AAA | 91-100 | Mortal pinnacle |
| S | Beyond the mortal cap | Transcendent rule breaker |
| SS | Demi-god authority | Reality warper governing a fundamental aspect |
| SSS | Conceptual embodiment | Living cosmic concept |

Do not assign SS or SSS a numerical level range. A skill's level measures expertise; character power and access conditions determine its usable effect. Read [Progression rules](progression-rules.md) for full conceptual catalysts, Authorities, and S/SS/SSS mechanics when creating an ascended character. This band table does not replace those rules. Use [Resolution rules](resolution-rules.md) for interactions across ranks.

## Attributes

Source: `ATTR.MECHANICS`.

| Attribute | Governs |
|---|---|
| STR | Melee damage, strength, intimidation |
| VIT | Maximum HP, physical and magical defense, poison and disease resistance |
| AGI | Speed, evasion, initiative, accuracy against swift targets |
| DEX | Precision, ranged attacks, trap disarming, crafting finesse, critical hit chance |
| INT | Spell potency, maximum MP, deciphering, tactical analysis |
| LCK | Fate, loot rarity, critical and miss odds, chance events |

The source provides no universal starting attribute array or HP/MP formula. Preserve a confirmed existing record. Use a descriptive profile when no numeric method is established. For a new numeric record, specify a world-compatible baseline and its method before approval; label it as a campaign convention. Never attribute invented numbers to the shared source.

## Procedural classes

Source: `CLASSGEN.DYNAMIC`.

Construct a class from archetype, modifier, and power source. Archetypes: Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Specialist, Hybrid. Modifiers include Elemental, Holy, Shadow, Tech, Beast, Cosmic, Ancient, and Modern. Sources include System, Divine, Scientific, Conceptual, and Forbidden. These are open pools. Name the resulting vocation to express the character's actual history and world, rather than printing its construction formula. Class rank is a separate field from character level.

Source `CLASSEVOL.RULE` requires both an approximate level threshold and a monumental class-related quest: first evolution around Level 40, second around Level 80. `HIDDENCLASS.TRIGGER` makes hidden classes rewards for resonant actions, unusual investment, talent-skill synergy, or world-specific encounters. Do not advertise hidden classes as ordinary initial options.

## Backstory weighting

Source: BACKSTORY.WEAVE. History contributes operative seeds, not only flavor. Connect past work to usable expertise, class direction, and a talent or skill proposal: medical practice can seed healing or precise anatomy-based strikes; programming can seed understanding of System logic; protecting another can seed Guardian or Hero directions. Use motivation as the primary weighting unless the selected wildcard applies.

Formative loss, betrayal, illness, or a dangerous transition can shape fears and trust when the player wants them represented. Propose those traits and any world-dependent mechanical consequence before acceptance. Do not diagnose trauma, impose a phobia, lower faction standing, or invent a numeric penalty from biography alone. The source names VIT/trust and reputation modifiers without amounts; retain an accepted trait's actual terms or record a campaign-specific proposal. This replaces compulsory trauma penalties with accepted character design while preserving meaningful history-to-build connections.

## Starting synthesis

Source: `NEXUSSYNTH.RULE`.

| Mode | Package |
|---|---|
| Standard | One thematically selected D-A talent |
| Resonance cascade | Two or three related F-C talents with synergy |
| Broken limiter | One talent deliberately overpowerful for its rank |
| Soul echo | One to three random Level 3-7 skills and past-life gear fragments |
| Conceptual awakening | One unique talent derived from the life or death story |

Choose one mode using accepted history, motivation, world, start, and [system concept](system-concepts.md). Do not silently combine all packages. The source `WILDCARD.NEXUSSYNTH.RULE` allows a 20% roll or narrative call to produce an unexpected package while retaining thematic fit. A wildcard suspends ordinary motivational weighting while retaining backstory resonance. It cannot erase an accepted defining promise. Starting synthesis is distinct from rewards earned later.

| Rank | Talent | Source effect |
|---|---|---|
| F | Appraisal | Inspect item and person statistics and descriptions |
| D | Inventory | Personal pocket dimension for item storage |
| C | Skill Steal | Copy skills from defeated enemies |
| B | Growth Boost | Accelerated EXP gain and leveling compared with natives |
| A | Modern Knowledge | Perfect recall of Earth knowledge; recreate modern technology and concepts |
| AA | Absolute Command | Commands bind anyone or anything of lower level or power |
| AAA | Time Loop | Return to a save point on death, with unlimited retries |
| S | Great Sage | Internal consciousness, tactical analysis, skill optimization, instant mastery |
| SS | Conceptual Manipulation | Manipulate abstract concepts |

Preserve these promised effects when selected. Define world interaction and actual access without quietly weakening them. For Time Loop, Author's Authority, or a save ability, read [Progression rules](progression-rules.md); ordinary saving a campaign record does not create an in-fiction ability.

## Transport-origin benefits

Source: `TRANSPORT.ORIGINS`. Apply only the accepted matching origin. These effects have separate provenance from synthesis; allocate each once. Do not infer a divine mistake or gaming accident merely from a whimsical system personality.

| Origin | Persistent benefit |
|---|---|
| Truck-kun | Karmic Compensation: once per arc, turn a critical failure into a critical success, cause an enemy miss, or find a needed item |
| Divine Summoning | Royal Recognition: Friendly +25 with the summoning kingdom, neutral toward its enemies; possible deity intervention |
| Gaming Accident | System Familiarity: hidden-stat visibility, strategy prediction, occasional developer functions |
| Divine Mistake | Compensation Package: two random talents, at least one F or D; periodic deity gifts |
| True Reincarnation | Soul Memory: +10 knowledge checks and recall of forgotten past-life skills |
| Portal Rift | Worldly Anchor: weak Earth connection, occasional Earth information, possible return path |

Use actual destination factions and accepted origin facts. Developer functions remain fictional abilities. Recruited Transplant is not automatically Divine Summoning. A personal training system is not automatically Gaming Accident.

## Rare manifestations

Source: `ULTRARARE.SYSTEM`, `ULTRARARE.TALENTS`, `ULTRARARE.CLASSES`, `ULTRARARE.GLITCHES`.

Ultra-rares are not offered directly. Unlock through a counter-intuitive action, extreme circumstance, or resonant backstory. Hidden variants can start under an ordinary identity and reveal progression later. Record the actual underlying state without spoiling its fiction.

| Pool | Retained source examples |
|---|---|
| Talents | F Perfect Internal Clock; D Narrative Inertia; A Semantic Drift; SSS Fourth Wall Peek |
| Classes | C Meme-Weaver; B Symbiotic Sovereign with colony consciousness; AA Chrono-Janitor |
| Glitches | Talent Cascade: five to ten random F/D talents instead of one powerful talent; Attribute Bleed: LCK influences other attributes |

Read the full rare-manifestation table in [Progression rules](progression-rules.md) before assigning effects. Chrono-Janitor does not grant time looping. Semantic Drift retains immense concentration and a high chance of bizarre failure. Do not invent a numerical rarity roll; the source gives none for the general ultra-rare pools. Deep End offers an unlock path, not a guaranteed initial ultra-rare grant.

## Skills and initial record

Sources: `SKILLPROG.SYSTEM`, `LEVELEXP.TABLE`, `STATUSWIN.FIELDS`.

Level 1 starts with at least three active skills. Soul-echo or retained-skill entries contribute to that minimum; do not count the same ability twice. The source gives no universal skill-level XP ladder. Record each skill's current level and usable effect explicitly.

| Evolution | Rule |
|---|---|
| Branch | A skill divides into specialized techniques |
| Fuse | Skills combine into a hybrid ability |
| Mutate | World and use transform the skill's function |
| Ascend | At skill Level 10, conceptual ascension becomes possible |

Creative use can earn hybrid abilities. Ascension is a possible evolution, not automatic unrestricted conceptual authority. Use the relevant source conceptual rules for its scope.

Initial record: Name, Race, Class and class tier, Level and character power, HP/MP, EXP and threshold, six attributes, talents with effects, skills with levels, and Titles. Include the chosen start's assets, relationships, obligations, seals, and access conditions in the character record. Source `STATUSWIN.FIELDS` lists the classic display fields; the complete record also includes talents, start details, and access conditions. A prose display can express equivalent facts. Evaluation prepares the record; arrival owns its first in-world reveal.

## Start precedence and recorded adaptations

Use [alternate starts](alternate-starts.md) for the nine-option identity and package, then the resolved world's selected entry for local arrival, currency, institutions, and usable power. Preserve confirmed continuing characters. Confirm a material conflict before replacing their state. Start assets and synthesis effects each have one owner; do not re-award assets during arrival.

| Issue | Rebuild authority |
|---|---|
| Hunter placement | Multiversal Hunter belongs to Transplant; Time Travel and Awakening belong to Regression |
| Contradictory old gear table | `ALTSTART.TABLE` supplies Hunter's B gear, 100+ Gold and four or five Level 7-10 skills; `STARTGEAR.ECONOMY` reverses variant labels. Use the shared alternate-starts helper and record the correction |
| Regression wording | `REGRESSION.NEXUSSYNTH.RULE` calls its Level 1 recovery premise Transplant. Route recovery through Time Travel or Awakening according to the accepted premise |

World-specific starting levels, bonuses, remembered techniques, and seals come from the complete supplied world, not a bundled destination rule. The transport-origin table above supplies separately owned origin benefits; an arbitrary portal description does not silently authorize every origin bonus.
