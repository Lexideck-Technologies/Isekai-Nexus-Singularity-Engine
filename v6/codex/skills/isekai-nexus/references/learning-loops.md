# Learning, cultivation, and recovery

Read the accepted character, complete world, [character rules](character-rules.md), [system terms](system-concepts.md), and [loop protocol](loop-protocol.md). Resolution owns attempts; progression owns advancement. This helper supplies learning evidence and local stage transitions.

## Academy and apprenticeship

Track enrollment or apprenticeship terms, instructor, learning objective, chosen method, practice conditions, feedback, and demonstrated application. Lessons introduce a usable exercise and a reason it matters. The student can ask, experiment, seek another instructor, change subjects, or leave under actual accepted terms. Enrollment alone grants no experience or mastery.

Use the world's institutions and actual calendar. Give teachers expertise, goals, and available attention; give peers agency. A practical result can create social consequences and research opportunities without requiring a rivalry or public humiliation. Setbacks yield specific feedback and a viable correction. An assessment measures demonstrated competence rather than forcing a preset spectacle.


## Cultivation

First locate the world's cultivation method. If none exists, propose a coherent discipline using its magic and the player's aims; record accepted effects and conditions before it changes character power. Do not transplant an unrelated realm ladder into every world.

| Local stage | Evidence | Result owner |
|---|---|---|
| Establish method | Available teacher, text, memory, or experiment; compatible resources | Cultivation records chosen method and conditions |
| Practice | Deliberate exercise with current capacity and useful feedback | Resolution determines attempt and costs |
| Understand | Observed relation between technique, body, power, or environment | Cultivation records demonstrated insight |
| Integrate | Repeatable application under a meaningful new condition | Progression evaluates a changed capability |
| Break through | Named prerequisite satisfied and transition attempted | Progression commits the earned change once |


Leveling increases capacity; cultivation changes control and form. One activity may justify both, but they require distinct evidence and effect identities. Training opportunity is not automatic instant mastery unless an accepted power actually grants it. Preserve exceptional abilities rather than inventing universal training caps to suppress them.

## Technique creation

Source: SKILLPROG.SYSTEM, SPONTANEOUS.AWAKENING, HIDDENCLASS.TRIGGER, CLASSGEN.DYNAMIC, CLASSEVOL.RULE. Use the full [progression helper](progression-rules.md) for actual awards and conditions.

Track desired effect, existing abilities, mechanism, attempted execution, observations, working scope, and the next useful experiment. Creative use receives a fitting development rather than a rote refusal. Separate proof of a useful trick from a completed high-tier ability. A technique's name cannot substitute for a defined effect and usable capacity.

Branch, Fuse, Mutate, and Ascend are routes from demonstrated use. A creative hybrid shares its component costs until a justified new rule is established; counting the same effect twice adds no power. Conceptual ascension requires the actual conceptual rules. Hidden classes respond to their source conditions, not every named invention.

## Personal systems

Use the accepted record as authority for trigger, promised benefit, costs, constraints, suspension, and recovery. Track eligibility, tasks actually offered, accepted tasks, evidence, fulfillment, and relationship response. Distinguish conditions the character can observe from deliberate mysteries. Check underlying eligibility consistently even when an interface is deceptive.

When a condition is met, provide the promised benefit. Guidance, a training opportunity, a reward, and a power each use their recorded mechanics. A conditional tutor can serve academy or cultivation practice; it adds no second automatic EXP event. End conditional access when its term ends without deleting unrelated earned skills. If the player declines an optional task, apply only a consequence actually contained in accepted terms. Personality cannot invent a new contract.

## Recovery and remembered futures

Track memory source, claimed past/future, current evidence, divergence, sealed ability, release condition, and recovered usable effect. A veteran can understand more than the present body can perform. Use remembered expertise to create useful approaches; preserve current resource and capacity conditions.

For each seal, establish a meaningful route: bodily development, compatible equipment, researched method, attunement, or a personal breakthrough. Fulfilled conditions release the named capability through progression once. Do not keep moving the goal after success. A time-travel start supplies knowledge from a particular history; actual divergence can invalidate forecasts. NPCs retain their own knowledge and reasons for belief.

Time resets require an accepted enabling ability and the actual SAVELOAD rules in progression-rules.md. Native Awakening does not move time backward. Campaign record maintenance does not grant an in-fiction rewind.

## Montage attachment data

Supply chosen learning objectives, methods, repeated exercises, practice limits, feedback sources, resource budgets, time horizon, current evidence, and stop events. Stop events include a breakthrough needing a choice, a new risky method, new patron terms, an important relationship decision, a release condition requiring action, or an unchosen assessment/expedition. Established low-stakes feedback can be compressed; substantive choices cannot.


World-specific disciplines, campus support, threat containment, and cultivation stages come from the full supplied world. Preserve their actual conditions rather than importing another destination's curriculum or power system.
