# Economy rules

Authority: preserved core CURRENCY, LOOT, LOOT.RANKMATCH, CRAFTING.SYSTEM, and STARTGEAR.ECONOMY. This document owns currency, crafting, and transactions. [Resolution rules](resolution-rules.md) owns the consolidated challenge/EXP/loot table. Adapt currency names to the resolved world and preserve its conversion; use the shared anchors where the world gives none. Anchors are reference prices, not universal quotes. Apply actual supply, agreed price, and the source Friendly 10% faction-shop discount once.

## Production and exchange

Record design, target rank, ingredient quantities, skill, facility, elapsed time, result, stock ownership, and transaction terms. Prove a prototype before repeatable production unless already established. Background knowledge supplies possible designs, not the Modern Knowledge talent or unavailable machinery/materials. Honor accepted talents and local capacity together.

Check every material, skill, and facility gate. A rank-A Master Lv10 OR unique Class/Talent satisfies the skill gate, not the A-material and master-workshop requirements. AA+ keeps rare materials, world-class talent or Divine Blessing, unique location, and the blueprint/ritual requirements. Generic recipes have no source time, output quantity, success chance, or cost formula. Establish actual quantities and method before committing a batch.

Shared Resolution adjudicates causal attempts and all cost proposals, committing only health, MP, physical conditions, and elapsed time. Economy commits inventory and funds movements from those same proposals once, including consumed ingredients/items, output stock, loot assignment, payment, and receipts. Never decrement inventory or funds again in Resolution. Progression owns noncombat advancement with demonstrated challenge/mastery; no automatic per-item XP. Sales proceeds are not a second profit reward. Stop a batch at budget/material/time/capacity limits or material choices.

Loot rank matches challenge rank, not protagonist rank. Luck operates within accepted terms; an ordinary success never guarantees catalogue loot. S+ items retain conceptual gates. Read Progression for EXP yields rather than duplicate awards.

Starting allocation belongs to [character rules](character-rules.md) and the [accepted character record](character-record.md). Carry the accepted currency, kit, advances, contracts, and allegiance into trade. Multiversal Hunter uses its accepted world-specific allocation under Transplant; continuing travelers retain their confirmed assets.

## Loot authority

Read the encounter award spine in [Resolution rules](resolution-rules.md) for every loot rank and its operative effect. Actual challenge rank determines loot rank; a goblin F encounter and an A dragon variant use their declared ranks, not contradictory species defaults. Economy owns acquisition, ownership, consumption, and exchange of that loot once. No second loot catalogue is maintained here.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| F | common F-tier mats | no skill required | none | campfire: [Firewood]+[Flint] |
