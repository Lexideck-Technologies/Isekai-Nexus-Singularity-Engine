# ISEKAI WORLD: Superearth Olympus

## World Classification

**World Type:** AAA Rank, Multi-Layered Realm. Planetary academies connect inhabited land, deep oceans, atmospheric sanctuaries, and sealed higher realms.
**Danger Level:** F to D in supervised campus grounds; C to A on regional expeditions; AA to AAA in the planetary frontier. S to SSS encounters belong to ascended journeys beyond the planetary wards.
**Power Ceiling:** SSS. Mortal mastery reaches Level 100 and AAA rank. Ascension opens planetary stewardship, interworld sovereignty, and conceptual achievement.

## World Overview

Olympus is a world so large that an island can contain more countries than an Earth traveler can name. Seventy-five percent of its surface is ocean. Two immense continents stand across that ocean, surrounded by archipelagos whose greatest islands rival Earth's continents. Mountain chains hold inland civilizations; their summits rise into inhabited cloud seas. A map of the whole planet hangs in every academy, and every map has unfinished margins.

The academies give this vast civilization its common language. Their graduates build harbor wards, teach village healers, negotiate passage with colossal beasts, chart cultivation paths, and maintain the ancient network that makes Olympus habitable. Every student learns enough to serve a community. A few become masters capable of raising mountain bridges or guiding a continental monsoon. Beyond those masters stand beings who crossed the mortal ceiling and returned with responsibilities as large as their power.

Your first morning can begin with a missing notebook, an awkward roommate, and a practical examination beside a pond. The pond contains a tiny doorway into an older ocean. The notebook belongs to someone from a continent you have never seen. A distant bell rings once beneath the earth. There is time to learn what each means. Olympus offers school friendships, competitive duels, field expeditions, original spellcraft, and a rising path into the deepest questions of its existence.

**Climate/Environment:** Equatorial reef belts, temperate archipelagos, immense continental interiors, seasonal sky rivers, polar glass seas, and inhabited deep-ocean thermal provinces. Habitable gravity is held by the ancient Measure, a planetary enchantment maintained through distributed observatories. Local weather follows terrain, currents, altitude, and seasonal heat. The world has ordinary day and night and a navigable sky.

## Geography & Major Locations

### The Planet and Its Roads

Olympus has a radius of approximately 63,710 kilometers, ten times Earth's radius. Surface area is approximately 51 billion square kilometers: 38.25 billion ocean and 12.75 billion land. Veyr and Orath each cover approximately four billion square kilometers. Islands and smaller landmasses occupy the remaining 4.75 billion. Individual islands reach tens of millions of square kilometers. Inland seas, watersheds, mountains, and climate belts divide the continents into many regions.

The Measure distributes gravitational attraction and holds surface weight near familiar human conditions. Its enchantments support the deep structural strata and require measurable maintenance. Local faults cause bounded weight anomalies. Students study stable demonstration chambers; ordinary campus life does not face gravitational collapse.

Walking and riding serve local settlements. Bellhaven lies six kilometers below the academy. Wind rails and regional sailcraft cover hundreds or thousands of kilometers over days. Skyships cross great islands in weeks. Ordinary ocean passages last months to years; current-riding ships shorten selected routes. Gates require surveyed addresses, maintained anchors, sufficient energy, and negotiated access. Continental interiors contain lifetime journeys for travelers without gates. Every expedition begins with a specific reachable destination.

### Caldris, the Starting Archipelago

- **Academy of the Open Horizon:** Hillside campus with twelve thousand students in foundation, professional, and advanced programs. The residential program begins at adulthood. Separate protected youth schools occupy another district. Its wards, workshops, and public services support a long campaign.
- **Bellhaven:** Harbor town of ferries, printers, communal kitchens, instrument makers, and guild offices. Its orchard receives Classic arrivals. The lower river district connects to the Time Travel future.
- **Vale Estate:** A walled research garden and patron residence two kilometers inland from Bellhaven. Lady Sereth receives scholarship applicants in its public instrument hall; private laboratories require invitation.
- **Practice Lake:** Controlled reservoir with floating gardens, rowing clubs, and maintenance chambers. Training creatures stay in supervised enclosures. A buried receiving gate belongs to its earlier history.
- **Lantern Marsh:** Wetland causeways, reed settlements, and a half-buried observatory. Surveys encounter F-to-C hazards and living ecosystems.
- **Meridian Anchorage:** Interworld reception station with Dema's exchange desk, anchor workshops, and protected guest quarters.
- **Glasswake Shoals:** Mineral reefs crossed by passage beasts. Licensed B-rank crews monitor offshore incursions. Nearshore boats remain in F-to-D waters.
- **Caldris Great Island:** Thirty million square kilometers of rainforests, mountains, inland seas, and hundreds of academies. Bellhaven occupies one coastal region.

### Veyr, the Eastern Mega Continent

- **Selen Fold:** Mountain basins and terraced settlements around inland seas. The central academy trains water arbiters and bridge cultivators.
- **Red Meridian:** Steppe routes, migrating sky herds, and wind observatories maintained by Arashi confederations.
- **Underbough:** Forest provinces surrounding immense root networks. Cairnwood councils exchange ecological knowledge with visiting scholars.
- **Aster Vault:** Observatory city built into a mountain reaching the sky rivers. The restricted summit holds a planetary Measure station.

### Orath, the Western Mega Continent

- **Luminous Basin:** Inland sea comparable to several Earth oceans in area, with glass-making cities and Pelassan shore academies.
- **Keth Emberlands:** Volcanic plateaus with managed geothermal fields, industrial schools, and body-tempering traditions.
- **Thousand-Step Interior:** Kingdoms, commons, desert libraries, and uncharted watersheds. An expedition studies one watershed; the interior contains many cultures.
- **Silent Bastion:** Sealed planetary station beneath a remote plateau. The Exile Regent controls its power-transfer machinery.

### Ocean, Sky, and Beyond

- **Namar Current Cities:** Mobile and anchored reef communities. Residents include air-breathers, trained water-breathers, and aquatic peoples.
- **Deep Cantons:** Inhabited thermal provinces beneath the ocean, linked by pressure-controlled harbors. Surface visitors need protection and invitations.
- **Glass Pole:** Polar ocean with suspended crystals retaining ancient seasonal measurements. AA expeditions study ruins beneath the ice.
- **Seven High Seas:** Atmospheric rivers carrying sky islands and migratory sanctuaries. Navigation requires compatible vessels and weather knowledge.
- **Far Crown:** Higher chambers reached through AAA observatories. Their space extends beyond the ordinary surface. Ascended travelers negotiate access.

## Cultures & Daily Life

Olympus contains thousands of societies. These seven establish reachable relationships and distinct foundations. Each includes dissent, mixed communities, and individuals whose choices depart from inherited expectations.

**Caldran Harbor Commons:** Stormy coasts reward cooperation in rescue, navigation, and storage. Merchant monopolies of safe landing prompted publicly funded harbor wards and elected councils. Sailcloth, orchards, instruments, and printed primers support commerce. Schools teach resonance by tending shared water systems. Cultivation emphasizes adaptable circulation and coordinated action. Communities argue over inland expansion and crowded coastal campuses. Caldrans import Selen stonework and trade with Namar; disagreements concern port access and ward maintenance. Public kitchens and lantern regattas make civic life visible to visitors.

**Selen Basin Leagues:** Mountain settlements depend on timed meltwater and bridges. Centuries of disputes produced basin courts, public measuring towers, and rotating arbitration apprenticeships. Terrace farming and precision stonework fund academies. Cultivation develops stable channels and sustained load-bearing magic. Houses cluster around stepped cisterns; spring festivals celebrate safe water release. Some leagues preserve hereditary engineering offices; reformers demand open examinations. Graduates work across Olympus, sometimes clashing with local authorities over water rights. Their respect for Caldran improvisation accompanies demands for better records.

**Namar Current Cities:** Reef cultivation, navigation, and living hulls sustain ocean communities. Seasonal reef failures established portable archives and citizenship that follows migrating households. Councils combine navigators, gardeners, and permanent-settlement delegates. Cultivation develops rhythm, pressure tolerance, and reciprocal bonds. Visitors dine in communal galley courts. Settled gardeners oppose reckless mobile harvesting; younger navigators favor deeper exploration. Namar carries academy correspondence and contests Pelassan toll claims. Surface and aquatic residents negotiate shared facilities rather than sharing identical needs.

**Arashi Road Confederations:** Steppes and sky-herd migrations require mobile settlements and durable instruments. Caravan defense treaties grew into federations of traveling camps, farming towns, and station cities. Route councils guarantee waystation access. Wind teaching begins with riding, instrument tuning, and weather observation. Cultivation joins movement and breath. Some communities inherit route leadership; others elect navigators each journey. Transport and atmospheric data support trade with Selen and Caldris. Confederations oppose exclusive academy control of sky roads. Mobile households and settled repair crews debate equitable maintenance contributions.

**Keth Emberland Assemblies:** Volcanic soil, geothermal heat, and gas pockets shape habitation. Eruption refugees founded assemblies that became permanent cities. Ceramics, metallurgy, greenhouses, and heat reservoirs support livelihoods. Workshop councils share authority with neighborhood assemblies. Arrays monitor vents; cultivation develops heat discipline and resilience. Apprentices learn emergency repair before prestige crafts. Mining cooperatives, researchers, and habitat advocates dispute expansion. Keth exports reliable focuses and relies on Pelassan shipping. Professors challenge traditions that praise endurance while ignoring preventable injury.

**Pelassan Shore Federation:** Salt farms, optics, fisheries, and shipping connect the Luminous Basin. Merchant wars led to a federation separating trade courts from elected governments. Colleges teach light measurement, memory instruments, and witnessed contract magic. Cultivation refines perception and accurate projection. Public disputations fill shaded glass halls. Dock cooperatives resist patents on foundational arrays; remote towns contest metropolitan dominance. Scholars exchange measurements across Olympus but dispute Namar toll exemptions and Keth runoff. Professional contracts specify obligations without controlling personal relationships.

**Cairnwood Root Councils:** Forest provinces contain canopy towns, ground farms, and underground communities. Overharvest disasters produced negotiated stewardship of root corridors. Neighborhoods, work associations, and long-lived forest partners sit on councils. Cultivation develops sensory integration, patient growth, and negotiated symbiosis. Grown supports, woven bark, and managed fungi shape daily craft. Conservation groups and expanding towns dispute priorities. Medicinal trade links them to Bellhaven; Selen engineers build bridges. Outsiders sometimes mistake stewardship debates for opposition to all change.

## Power Systems & Magic

### Resonance and Array Craft

Olympus carries the Deep Radiance. Bodies accumulate mana from it; practiced channels shape effects. Instruments and arrays provide repeatable patterns. Novices heat cups and redirect water ribbons. Masters sustain city wards, open surveyed passages, and redirect storms within their actual tier.

**Casting Method:** Elemental Channeling through gesture, breath, voice, or concentration; Runic/Array Magic through constructed patterns. Compatible focuses provide System-Assisted execution for learned spells.
**Mana Source:** Personal MP replenished through recovery and compatible ambient mana. Arrays require stored charge or maintained supplies. Strong surroundings do not remove personal limits.
**Skill Requirements:** Relevant skills, concentration, and action-appropriate attributes. INT supports comprehension; DEX supports construction; VIT supports bodily practice. Affinity influences methods and difficulty without prescribing a lifelong discipline.

Spells have defined effects, costs, and ranges. Arrays take preparation, materials, and upkeep. Overchanneling causes recognizable exhaustion and channel injury. Counterspells affect identified patterns. A clever low-tier solution can exploit a mechanism without gaining higher-tier destructive force.

### Cultivation of the Inner Horizon

Cultivation develops the relationship between body and mana through breath, circulation, perception, reflection, and tested practice. Listening, Circulation, Integration, and Personal Horizon name areas of development rather than replacement level bands. Students revisit them at increasing depth.

Listening distinguishes personal channels from surrounding resonance. Circulation supports reliable mana use. Integration connects body, focus, and disciplines. Personal Horizon establishes a repeatable signature approach from demonstrated insight. Schools offer foundations; students combine traditions or establish new paths through clear results and costs.

Practice consumes time and effort under suitable conditions. Materials support techniques when their properties apply. Repeating a solved exercise maintains familiarity; meaningful improvement requires new difficulty, finer execution, or discovery. A breakthrough changes a stated capability when its conditions are fulfilled and grants the corresponding benefit once.

### Patrons, Bonds, and Ascension

Spirits negotiate limited blessings with named terms, effects, and sources. Bonds with creatures require reciprocal care and willing participation. Neither creates an unchosen obligation.

Qualified ascendants beyond the mortal ceiling study the Far Crown. S-rank stewardship concerns planetary systems; SS reaches interworld structures; SSS Conceptual Weaving acts on understood axioms. Historical knowledge grants no automatic access to those powers. Advancement follows the shared conceptual progression and its prerequisites.

## Growth & Adventure Pacing

Leveling expands usable capacity through accomplishments. Cultivation improves control, integration, and the forms that capacity takes. An expedition can advance both: rescue earns its challenge rewards, while maintaining circulation under moving-water pressure develops a technique. Apply each distinct benefit once. A victory receives one EXP award and one loot resolution; calling it coursework adds no duplicate payment.

Use the shared level progression, attribute and skill awards, EXP thresholds, milestone benefits, and mortal-to-conceptual transition. Olympus supplies opportunities without replacing values. Level 10 can reveal a signature talent during a practical. Levels 25, 50, 75, and 100 retain their exact shared milestone awards. Level 90 supports apex awakening. School year, cultivation insight, and level remain distinct.

Courses include Practical Resonance, Living Arrays, Navigation, Field Medicine, Material Studies, Comparative Cultivation, and Civic Wardcraft. Each offers lessons, partner work, projects, and elective challenges. Clubs tend gardens, race sailcraft, stage illusions, build instruments, and investigate history. Failed examinations produce feedback and repair opportunities rather than automatic campaign termination.

The distant **Unmeasured Hunger** is a SSS entity beyond the Far Crown that consumes relationships holding stable worlds together. Nine independent Crown Locks prevent its entry. Their sources are redundant and distributed; routine upkeep survives individual failures. Academies maintain this network because no individual or government can observe and repair the whole planet.

Early signs include impossible instrument weights, contradictory current charts, and shared dreams near tampered stations. Local faults produce bounded problems. Escalation requires concrete events: theft of an authenticated key, diversion of station charge, or an experiment breaching a higher chamber. Record the event and its consequences. Ordinary study, travel, and chosen downtime never cause automatic lock failure. Years of friendship, discovery, and mastery can precede planetary confrontation.

The Hunger reaches only compromised higher chambers and opened channels. Restoring a channel removes that route. Research, repair, and diplomacy can contain the conflict without compulsory battle against its highest form. A local career, continental adventure, or ascended journey each supports a complete campaign.

## Signature Quests

### Repeatable Activities

- **Garden Practicals:** Stabilize changing conditions. Earn challenge-appropriate EXP, course credit, and demonstrated technique development.
- **Field Surveys:** Map a named causeway, watershed, or reef. Receive contracted payment, data access, and standing.
- **Duel Circles:** Supervised matches use declared rules and appropriate opponents. Recognition and insight follow actual results.
- **Workshop Commissions:** Repair specific instruments for payment. Copying solved designs gives no repeated discovery reward.
- **Current Watches:** Join licensed coastal crews, record weather, and manage suitable hazards for wages and navigation practice.

### Major Quest Lines

**"A Timetable of Your Own"**

Choose courses, form a project group, and complete a public demonstration. Orren can become a rival, friend, or collaborator. The misplaced flowstone opens an optional investigation while the school term continues.

- *Rewards:* Recognized study plan, workshop access, relationships, and challenge-appropriate progression. Choose whether to pursue the observatory apprenticeship.

**"The Bell Beneath the Lake"**

A repair exposes an earlier receiving chamber. Read its maps, reconcile maintenance records, and trace Bellhaven's river system. Neglected infrastructure and tampering threaten local flooding. Time travelers recognize part of the chain; others discover it through evidence.

- *Rewards:* Lakewise or an advanced restoration title, paid civic work, history, and improved safety.
- *Stakes:* Failed repairs affect a named district and prompt further response. The town and academy continue to act. A student's setback does not summon the Hunger.

**"Six Shores, One Instrument"**

Gather complementary techniques from Caldris, Selen, Namar, Arashi, Keth, and Pelassa. Choose an itinerary and negotiate reciprocal contributions. Regional quests develop each relationship. Build an instrument that measures different traditions without forcing them into one school.

- *Rewards:* Signature focus, exchange affiliations, original research, and a Personal Horizon opportunity.
- *Stakes:* Appropriation and broken agreements damage specific relationships. Repair requires restitution or new agreements.

**"The Regent's Borrowed Crown"**

Bellhaven access forgery reveals stolen calibration records and covert excavations. C-to-B investigation leads into continental journeys and AA-to-AAA station work. Expose the supply network before choosing negotiation, dismantlement, or confrontation.

- *Rewards:* Station fellowship, recovered resources, advanced cultivation research, and Far Crown access.
- *Stakes:* Specific thefts create documented faults. Successful containment provides lasting stability.

**"A Horizon That Holds"**

Ascended characters investigate why the locks require independent stewards. Form a coalition, restore routes, and develop understanding adequate to oppose the Hunger. Permanent containment, a renewed covenant, and confrontation beyond the locks are distinct outcomes.

- *Rewards:* Earned ascension achievements, interworld responsibilities, and a stable planetary future.
- *Stakes:* Actually opened channels determine the Hunger's reach. Close one and return to planetary life before undertaking the ultimate confrontation.

## Unique Superearth Olympus Features

### The Academy as a Living Place

Residences organize by chosen cohorts and practical needs. Students change courses, establish clubs, work for stipends, publish arguments, and invite travelers to teach. No permanent school assignment determines personality or future discipline. Rivalries involve real ideas and opportunities.

The Lantern Regatta, research fair, exchange arrivals, and winter instrument market provide ordinary stories and larger connections. A promise kept to a friend remains meaningful after immense power is earned.

### Vastness with Reachable Layers

Travel uses actual routes, destinations, and means. Reveal regions through ecology, settlements, and people. Preserve distance, correspondence delays, supplies, and discoveries. New gates require surveyed anchors and maintained agreements. Power opens larger journeys while existing communities retain their lives.

The Great Survey records observations rather than declaring the planet fully known. Trustworthy coastlines border unexplored interiors. New cultures grow from environment, history, livelihood, institutions, and practice.

### The Measure and Its Keepers

Weight orchards grow fruit that registers gravity drift. Measurewrights compare harvests with station records. Villages host them as essential civic workers. Students can contribute useful observations before understanding the whole network.

Crown Locks connect diverse sources because redundancy creates resilience. Exceptional individuals become stewards; planetary stability still depends on communities maintaining their contributions.

## Achievement System

### Student and Citizen Achievements

- **F, First Clear Note:** Complete a practical through an explainable, repeatable technique.
- **D, A Place at the Table:** Sustain a contribution to a cohort, workshop, or community.
- **C, Lantern Cartographer:** Verify a regional survey used by others.
- **B, Your Own Horizon:** Demonstrate a personal cultivation method with defined effects and safe limits.

### Explorer and Master Achievements

- **A, Six Shores Scholar:** Complete reciprocal research across six named cultural networks.
- **AA, Keeper of a Living Route:** Restore and maintain a major habitat or transport system with its residents.
- **AAA, The Crown Returned:** End the Regent's station control and restore legitimate stewardship.

### Ultimate Achievements

- **S, A World Held Lightly:** Earn planetary stewardship through sustained contribution respecting local agency.
- **SS, The Unbroken Passage:** Stabilize an interworld route without displacing its inhabitants.
- **SSS, A Horizon That Holds:** Establish permanent conceptual containment or defeat the Hunger in its domain through earned understanding and power.
- **Home Beyond Power:** Return freely to a chosen community after ascension. Recognizes a chosen conclusion without granting another combat tier.
