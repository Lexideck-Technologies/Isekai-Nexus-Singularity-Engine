# ISEKAI WORLD: Cherrywood High

## World Classification

**World Type:** F-Rank - Mundane Earth 2.0 (Social Drama Variant)  
**Danger Level:** Minimal physical threats, maximum social complexity  
**Power Ceiling:** D-Tier (Human social influence, academic achievement, athletic performance)  
**Special Note:** **Otome Thematic Overlay Active** - Romance and relationships are the primary progression system

---

## World Overview

Cherrywood High appears to be an ordinary American high school, but for the transported soul, it becomes an intricate social battleground where every interaction carries weight and every relationship shapes destiny. This massive H-shaped institution houses over a thousand students across four stories, creating a labyrinthine social ecosystem where cliques, crushes, and academic pressures create drama more intense than any dragon's lair.

Here, power comes not from magic or combat prowess, but from charisma, intelligence, athletic ability, and social standing. A well-timed joke can defeat a bully more effectively than any sword, and the right alliance can open doors that brute force never could. Your greatest enemy might be the upcoming SATs, and your most valuable treasure could be a heartfelt love letter.

**Unique Feature:** All progression is social, academic, or romantic. Combat is replaced by social conflicts, reputation management, and relationship building.

### Classic - "New Transfer Student"

**Arrival:** You awaken in your family's car as it pulls into Cherrywood High's front parking lot. Your "transfer paperwork" is complete, but you're starting mid-semester with no established friendships, no knowledge of the social hierarchy, and no idea which lunch table to sit at. Everyone will be curious about the mysterious new student.
**Prior Experience:** A mid-semester transfer with previous-life knowledge and no learned Cherrywood hierarchy.
**Current Power:** Level 1 for a new character; preserve accepted existing level and usable powers. All listed equipment keeps its promised functions.
**Starting Kit:** Backpack with basic school supplies, $20 lunch money, class schedule, locker combination  
**Initial Challenges:** Finding classes, avoiding social faux pas, establishing your place in the hierarchy  
**First Contact:** A helpful student council member offers to show you around, or you accidentally bump into someone important
**Relationships:** Family delivers you; a student council guide or accidental acquaintance is your first school contact.
**Obligations:** Attend classes and find your locker while learning the social hierarchy.
**First Opportunity:** Ask for a tour, check your schedule or choose a lunch table and introduce yourself.
**Growth Path:** Build authentic friendships and develop club, academic or athletic skills.

### Recruited Transplant - "The Exchange Student of Destiny"

**Arrival:** You've been sent here by a cosmic force of narrative causality to ensure this year's story reaches its "perfect ending." Your destiny is to unite the cliques, expose a brewing scandal before it ruins lives, and ensure the Prom is a night of genuine joy, not heartbreak. **Current Power:** Level 1 for a new character; preserve accepted existing level and usable powers. All listed equipment keeps its promised functions.

### Multiversal Hunter - "The Senior Who's Seen It All"

**Arrival:** You enter Cherrywood from other worlds as a veteran of school dramas, seeking a normal year or a chosen scandal investigation.
**Prior Experience:** Retain your actual multiversal career and learned destination facts. Similar worlds do not reveal this world's secrets, future or hidden names.
**Current Power:** A new Hunter defaults to Level 31, B-rank usable capacity; an existing traveler keeps the accepted level, powers and stronger assets. All retained expertise and artifact functions remain available under their stated conditions.
**Starting Kit:** You arrive with a designer wardrobe (B-Tier), an untraceable trust fund (100+ Gold equivalent), and skills like `[Social Manipulation] Lv. 8` and `[Clique Infiltration] Lv. 7`. Your [Dimensional Anchor] is a locker that opens to a pocket dimension containing your resources. Add [Conflict Mediation] Lv. 9 and [Academic Strategy] Lv. 8. These are usable social expertise; the B-Tier wardrobe does not grant superhuman violence.
**Relationships:** No new recruiter debt or rookie reset. Preserve accepted local contacts; new faction standing follows actual introductions.
**Obligations:** Choose the pursuit and respect local access conditions. The locker still opens to the resource pocket dimension. Return travel needs a keyed destination and a charged opening; stored-resource access does not create unlimited world passage.
**First Opportunity:** Read the current school newspaper, meet a willing club representative or inspect your anchor locker.
**Growth Path:** Use retained expertise to investigate the chosen threat and grow through local discoveries, training and completed work.


### Time Travel - "The Senior Who's Seen It All"

**Arrival:** You return to the first morning of senior year, before the cheating scandal that expelled your friend.
**Prior Experience:** You remember tests, breakups and scandals from the earlier senior year, including the evidence mishandled before your friend's expulsion.
**Current Power:** Level 1 with one +5 bonus to each of STR, VIT, AGI, DEX, INT and LCK. This includes and replaces any partial attribute bonus described in your remembered start; never add that earlier bonus again. Learned skill levels do not restore former full-body capacity.
**Starting Kit:** Old unfashionable clothes and part-time job money; [Persuasion] Lv. 5 and [Gossip Analysis] Lv. 4.
**Relationships:** Preserve the earlier self's actual relationships. Explain apparent sudden competence through evidence; NPCs detect temporal residue only with abilities that can detect it.
**Obligations:** Choose whether to change the remembered disaster; foreknowledge follows the earlier timeline and may lose accuracy when events diverge.
**First Opportunity:** Ask Ms. Ramirez how to preserve evidence, check a remembered early rumor or reconnect with your friend.
**Growth Path:** Develop present capacity while retaining expertise and change the remembered outcome through reachable actions.
**World Response:** Teachers and old friends may notice sudden maturity after the summer. Subtly guide events without assuming their agreement; remembered tests, breakups and scandals remain knowledge of the earlier year.

### Awakening - "The Voice Remembered at Lunch"

**Arrival:** You awaken in the present senior semester as memories of a former successful school life surface; no school year is repeated.
**Prior Experience:** Memories and expertise of the accepted former life return; these memories do not prove a time reversal.
**Current Power:** Level 1 usable capacity with the working technique stated below. Former high powers remain sealed; remembered advanced expertise is retained without automatically becoming full output.
**Starting Kit:** Ordinary school clothes, notebook, old speech draft and a basic phone.
**Relationships:** Seek the named local mentors through actual contact; retain accepted present relationships rather than implanting former-life bonds.
**Obligations:** Earn consent and trust; remembered relationships are not present-day Affinity awards.
**First Opportunity:** Practice listening with a willing peer, ask Mr. Thompson about debate or review the old speech.
**Growth Path:** Former reputation and exceptional social command are sealed behind a new identity and unproven trust. A working [Active Listening] Lv. 1 helps one willing classmate clarify a concern. Recover influence through honest conversations, debate practice and earned club responsibility, with no magical control over classmates.

## The Social Ecosystem

| Social Tier | Groups | Influence Level | Special Abilities |
|-------------|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **A-Tier** | **Popular Elite** (Cheerleaders, Star Athletes, Prom Royalty) | School-wide influence, teacher favoritism | Can make or break reputations, access to exclusive events |

- *Mystery Elements:* Gathering clues, interviewing suspects, uncovering motives
- *Social Stakes:* Academic integrity, college applications, trusted relationships

**"The Unity Campaign"**  
When bullying reaches a crisis point, organize a school-wide anti-bullying initiative that brings together students from all social levels.

- *Leadership Challenge:* Uniting opposed groups, changing school culture, inspiring real change

**"The Senior Year Countdown"**
Navigate the emotional complexity of final year: college applications, changing friendships, first love, and the approaching end of an era.
