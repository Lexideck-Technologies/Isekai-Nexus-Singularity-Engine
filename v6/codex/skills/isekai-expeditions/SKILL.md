# Expeditions

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus/references/loop-protocol.md), [adventure records](../isekai-nexus/references/adventure-loops.md), current world/site, character, party agreements, and scene. Use the common silent output frame.

Activate for approaching or traversing a bounded hazardous site. Own expedition position, mapped links, obstacle evidence, and checkpoints. Site tier and character tier remain distinct; a world's broad classification never makes every chamber its maximum threat.

1. Establish the chosen objective, known entrance, route, party roles, supply expectations, and withdrawal conditions. Record actual entry after it occurs. Do not treat inspecting an entrance as consenting to enter.
2. Scout the present chamber or floor through declared actions. Read [world and dungeon rules](../isekai-nexus/references/world-rules.md) for relevant structure and the world-specific site for actual conditions. Convey assessable signs of hazards, paths, and useful resources.
3. Pass chosen traversal, trap interaction, negotiation, or encounter to [Resolution](../isekai-resolution/SKILL.md). Combat is one approach. Preserve effective abilities and clever plans under source agency and competence rules; do not fabricate universal floor seals or power resets.
4. Record the specific obstacle's result, opened links, party condition, and available recovery. Submit threat/loot/advancement evidence to their shared owners and [Progression](../isekai-progression/SKILL.md). A floor clears only when its actual clearance condition is met.
5. Offer proceeding, recovery, another route, or exit according to the resulting position. Stop at the player's decision. Preserve map and checkpoint state across interruption and reentry; repopulation or changed architecture requires an established site rule or concrete event.

Use Exploration for surrounding routes, Missions for contract evidence, Survival for urgent adversity, and Multiversal Travel for an actual world crossing. Local floors and planar chambers do not automatically reset character creation. Expose repeatable traversals, supplies/time basis, encounter evidence, and stop events for Montage; novel choices and dangerous encounters remain decision boundaries.

Read the [Codex play contract](../isekai-nexus/references/codex-play-contract.md) when entering play or restoring a campaign after context loss.
