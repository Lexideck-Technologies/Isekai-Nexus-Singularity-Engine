## d-references-social-rules-md

# Social rules

Authority: preserved core ENTITIES, SOCIAL, and COMPANIONS. Authoritative adapted catalogues follow. Adapt names to the world; rank describes capacity and never mandates every archetype. Preserve NPC identity, goals, knowledge, commitments, and agency. Keep individual affinity, faction reputation, and public-image Reputation separate. Resolve actual interactions through Shared Resolution; record evidence for every change once. Progression owns EXP and earned abilities.

## Adjudication

Allied NPCs supply the possible companion pool; recruitment still follows its method and agreement. The S-rank Keeper of Cosmic Balance is also the granting entity for the source Chrono-Janitor rare class. That connection opens an opportunity under the class's actual gates and never awards the class for meeting the Keeper. The SSS Outer God shares the source Abstract Horror's resistance to conventional combat; route an encounter through its actual threat rules.

Elegance, Charisma, public Reputation, and social Intellect are GM-tracked and revealable through an accepted Appraisal talent. Social Intellect measures political acumen; preserve its connection to Diplomatic domain development without silently replacing the character's INT attribute.

Love motivation opens opportunities without forced romance. Activate the court overlay for chosen courtship or sustained social maneuvering. Its 3-5 cast members remain independent people; introduce them through scenes. Adapt gender and orientation to the player and world. Romance involves adults and mutual consent. Affection, rescue, rank, gifts, and contracts never substitute for consent. Platonic/familial bonds share the affinity spine. Intimate scenes focus on emotional intimacy and remain non-explicit. Establish consent before darker or intimate themes. Do not import private agent personas.

Rescue provides the source Friendly(+15) recruitment opportunity; joining requires voluntary agreement. This explicitly replaces source automatic recruitment. Loyalty descriptions express inclination, not compelled sacrifice. Magical bonds follow accepted terms. An inactive network stays available; do not erase it or degrade affinity simply because the player chooses another loop. Establish an actual neglected expectation and meaningful elapsed time before relationship decay.

The source gives faction bands and recruitment seeds but no universal reputation delta formula or numeric affinity thresholds. Preserve its qualitative relationship stages. Record a campaign convention if numerical adjudication is required; never invent canonical thresholds. NPC response follows personality, evidence, and knowledge; persuasion is not guaranteed agreement.

Choose and record an active companion cap of 3 or 4; the source leaves the choice open. The inactive network is unlimited. Divide earned base party EXP among all active members once, then apply each companion affinity rate and the protagonist Growth Boost only to the protagonist share. Progression owns award and rounding convention; source rounding is unspecified. Loot follows an actual party distribution agreement through economy. Coaching, equipment, and potions support native growth with evidence; one outcome grants no duplicate EXP.

## Companion Limit

Active party: record a cap of 3 or 4 companions for narrative focus. Inactive network: unlimited, available for specific tasks. Maintaining high affinity involves meaningful attention, gifts, and shared experiences. Decay requires actual neglected expectations and meaningful elapsed time.

## Party Rules

Divide base EXP among all active members. Apply Growth Boost only to the protagonist share; apply companion affinity rates to their shares through Progression. Party leadership distributes loot under the recorded agreement. Allies usually cooperate; neutral mercenaries can refuse dangerous orders without additional pay. Followers grow at native rates; coaching, high-quality loot, and rare potions can accelerate growth with evidence.

# Economy rules

Authority: preserved core CURRENCY, LOOT, LOOT.RANKMATCH, CRAFTING.SYSTEM, and STARTGEAR.ECONOMY. This document owns currency, crafting, and transactions. [Resolution rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-resolution-rules-md) owns the consolidated challenge/EXP/loot table. Adapt currency names to the resolved world and preserve its conversion; use the shared anchors where the world gives none. Anchors are reference prices, not universal quotes. Apply actual supply, agreed price, and the source Friendly 10% faction-shop discount once.

## Production and exchange

Record design, target rank, ingredient quantities, skill, facility, elapsed time, result, stock ownership, and transaction terms. Prove a prototype before repeatable production unless already established. Background knowledge supplies possible designs, not the Modern Knowledge talent or unavailable machinery/materials. Honor accepted talents and local capacity together.

Check every material, skill, and facility gate. A rank-A Master Lv10 OR unique Class/Talent satisfies the skill gate, not the A-material and master-workshop requirements. AA+ keeps rare materials, world-class talent or Divine Blessing, unique location, and the blueprint/ritual requirements. Generic recipes have no source time, output quantity, success chance, or cost formula. Establish actual quantities and method before committing a batch.

Shared Resolution adjudicates causal attempts and all cost proposals, committing only health, MP, physical conditions, and elapsed time. Economy commits inventory and funds movements from those same proposals once, including consumed ingredients/items, output stock, loot assignment, payment, and receipts. Never decrement inventory or funds again in Resolution. Progression owns noncombat advancement with demonstrated challenge/mastery; no automatic per-item XP. Sales proceeds are not a second profit reward. Stop a batch at budget/material/time/capacity limits or material choices.

Loot rank matches challenge rank, not protagonist rank. Luck operates within accepted terms; an ordinary success never guarantees catalogue loot. S+ items retain conceptual gates. Read Progression for EXP yields rather than duplicate awards.

Starting allocation belongs to [character rules](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-rules-md) and the [accepted character record](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-record-md). Carry the accepted currency, kit, advances, contracts, and allegiance into trade. Multiversal Hunter uses its accepted world-specific allocation under Transplant; continuing travelers retain their confirmed assets.

## d-references-domain-rules-md

# Domain rules

Authority: preserved core DOMAIN.UNLOCK, TERRITORY.TIERS, DOMAIN.DEVELOP, DOMAIN.CHALLENGES. Authoritative tables follow. Ordinary household life and helping neighbors require no domain unlock. A domain means actual territorial or organizational authority.

## Gates and state

Either Level 25+ OR a completed major quest granting land/title/organizational leadership unlocks the domain layer. Allied/Revered faction standing supports a qualifying quest; it never replaces either gate. Eligibility does not grant land, followers, or revenue. Establish actual possession/recognized leadership and commitments. Record unlock proof, authority basis, tier, population, resources, income, costs, projects, delegated roles, treaties, challenges, and accounting periods.

## Development and consequences

Territory income uses Silver at F-D and Gold at C-AA under the shared currency conversion. AA legendary artifacts remain AA+ loot and crafting capacity; owning a kingdom does not automatically generate an artifact.

Choose an objective and focus. Check its listed skill/talent/knowledge requirement; establish labor, financing, materials, approvals, time, and completion evidence. Shared Resolution adjudicates causal attempts and all cost proposals, committing only health, MP, physical conditions, and elapsed time. Domain owns projects, authority commitments, territory state, tier eligibility, and accounting-period evidence. Economy commits each inventory/funds movement from the same proposals once. Economy applies receipts/payments once; Social owns faction/individual changes; Progression owns earned abilities/titles.

Tier income bands are monthly reference income, not immediate rewards or guaranteed net profit. Record actual income and basis; account for actual upkeep/disruption. Source gives no universal taxation/expense formula. Apply income once per completed month or a previously accepted proration convention. Overlapping scenes and montage cannot collect it repeatedly. Population and functioning capabilities support tier advancement; personal Level 25 never turns an outpost into a kingdom. Source tiers end at AA; higher tiers need accepted world rules.

Challenges arise from actual resource balance, rivals, threats, or delegation. Never impose invasion/betrayal solely because time passes. Distant threats keep their actual timeline; ordinary downtime never causes automatic doom. Establish succession/delegated authority where relevant. Sovereignty/Dominion authorities use accepted conceptual terms and domain scaling; political rank grants no missing conceptual power.
