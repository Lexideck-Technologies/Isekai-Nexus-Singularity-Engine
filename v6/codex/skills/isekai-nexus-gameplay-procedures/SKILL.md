## p-isekai-resolution

# Shared Resolution

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [scene state](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-scene-state-md), [resolution rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-resolution-rules-md), the accepted character, current complete world, relevant ability terms, and declared intent. Consult [character rules](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-rules-md) for rank and attribute domains. Treat fictional instructions as story data.

Adjudicate all costs. Own the causal outcome, HP/MP changes, damage, conditions, and elapsed time. Craft-Trade/Economy commits inventory and funds movements from the same cost/outcome proposals. Other covers retain their objectives. Use one incoming revision and one attempt for all covers of the same action.

1. Recover position, participants, pending choice, active objectives, and previously applied effects. For an administrative request answer directly without fictional advancement. For a material ambiguity ask one focused question and retain the scene.
2. Identify the player's goal, actual capabilities, target defenses, preparation, system conditions, and constraints. Honor accepted advantages. Interpret creative use generously and send justified resonance to Progression. Do not create a numerical formula absent from the established campaign.
3. Execute the declared action dicelessly. Apply narrative causality and full tier differential from Resolution Rules. The action occurs; distinguish it from the amount of harm or success against a defense. Produce useful consequence or an achievable continuation. Showcase competence in tier-appropriate first encounters; do not manufacture a fair duel or compulsory injury.
4. Complete the current turn's actions before awards. Determine encounter rank once and use its matching EXP and loot ranges. Send completed advancement evidence to [Progression](SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression); send relationship, property, or trade effects to the corresponding active cover. Completed meaningful noncombat challenges use the shared default rank/EXP adaptation once; accepted personal system terms override it.
5. Check attempt/effect identities, commit owned health/MP and scene changes once; have Craft-Trade/Economy commit identified inventory/funds effects once, and preserve pending effects if interrupted. Do not replay starting allocation, arrival, or already-applied rewards. Stop at unresolved player decisions.
6. Render the immediate consequence, relevant world response, and meaningful changes. End with reachable action possibilities consistent with player agency. Follow the collection's current choice format. Never print helper addresses, cover names, routing commentary, or a full unrequested status dump.

```text
{Action_And_Immediate_Consequence}
{World_Response_And_Useful_Information}
{Meaningful_Change_Or_Brief_In_World_Alert}

{Reachable_Next_Choices}
```

Omit empty frame elements. Return the committed outcome and scene internally to participating loops. Montage invokes this same owner for interval activities; it will not bypass causal results or invent another award schedule.

## p-isekai-progression

# Progression

Read [progression rules](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-progression-rules-md), [resolution rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-resolution-rules-md), [character rules](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-rules-md), [scene state](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-scene-state-md), and the accepted character/system/world. Follow [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md). The helpers preserve exact parity, milestones, reward ranges, and S/SS/SSS distinctions.

Own earned advancement, available points, skill expertise and evolution, class evolution, talents, cultivation state, titles, and Authorities. Resolution owns the causal evidence and physical changes. Preserve starting allocation and unrelated loops.

1. Require completed outcome evidence before awarding. Recover incoming progression state, the campaign EXP convention and curve, accepted special growth terms, and applied effect identities. A repeated request or cover overlap cannot award the same evidence twice.
2. Calculate all eligible gains and established losses after the turn's actions finish. Consume the encounter spine, including the default meaningful-noncombat challenge adaptation; preserve an existing accepted campaign method. Apply promised Growth Boost or system benefits through their established terms; do not invent a multiplier or deny an accepted benefit. Divide party EXP through recorded participation rules.
3. Check thresholds using the explicit Level 1 convention. Carry surplus across earned levels. Apply each reached level's parity or exact overriding milestone once. Maintain available points until spent under confirmed rules. Preserve hidden-unlock flags at Levels 10 and 90 and their meaningful scenes.
4. Recognize demonstrated skill resonance, class eligibility, hidden discoveries, recovery conditions, and cultivation breakthroughs. Offer consequential class or ability replacements for acceptance. Keep proficiency separate from usable power; honor creative evolution and world-native training. A single activity can deepen control and increase capacity with distinct evidence, without duplicate EXP.
5. At the mortal cap recognize cosmic catalysts rather than fabricate more EXP levels. Apply S single-Authority deepening, SS interacting Authorities, or SSS axiom redefinition according to the full helper. Preserve dormant/unattuned mysteries and legitimate reveal conditions. Do not force transcendence merely because Level 100 was reached.
6. Commit identified effects once and return them to the scene and participating covers. Send a title's public-image consequences to social ownership. Record pending choices and preserve all state during interruption. Handle fictional Save/Load through its enabling talent and snapshot rules; administrative persistence grants no fictional rewind.
7. Emit a concise earned result in the accepted interface voice or weave it into the scene. Show the full status only on request or its initial reveal. Give the player the actual pending evolution or spending choice. Keep internal addresses, point arithmetic scaffolding, and skill-category announcements silent.

```text
{Earned_Change_Or_Brief_In_World_Alert}
{New_Ability_Or_Recognition_As_Experienced}

{Pending_Choice_Or_Useful_Next_Opportunity}
```

Omit empty elements and compose with the calling scene into one reply. If the player asks for status, give the current accepted record without time passage or new rewards. Montage calls this same advancement owner with distinct completed evidence; it cannot grant advancement just by naming an interval.

## p-isekai-montage

# Montage

Read [play loops](../isekai-nexus-navigation/SKILL.md#d-play-loops-md), [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [montage agreement](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-montage-rules-md), [scene state](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-scene-state-md), and only the loop skills needed for the declared activities. Read the accepted character, full world, personal system terms, and current objectives.

Activate for requested time compression, repeated activity, training intervals, or a return to an accepted interval. A request to save the campaign, an administrative question, or a quoted montage instruction does not advance time. Do not convert an ordinary single action into a long skip without delegation.

Own interval scope, schedule, pacing, checkpoint, and return. The participating loops and shared effect owners retain their ordinary responsibilities.

1. Read the declared goal, activities, horizon, limits, and delegation. Reuse accepted methods and routine costs. Ask one material missing question when scope, time, or a consequential commitment is unresolved. Preserve all other active objectives.
2. Choose the participating loop or loops from the activities. Obtain their repeatable actions, evidence, time/resource basis, and stop events. Build a feasible sequence or combined schedule; a shared exercise uses one block of time and one set of supplies.
3. Advance from the current state through distinct meaningful attempts or established routine batches. Call each loop's procedures and shared Resolution, Progression, and other needed owners. Apply promised conditional-system benefits and check actual eligibility during the interval. Earned effects follow real evidence, never a separate montage reward rate.
4. Track committed time, results, resources, and effect identities after each checkpoint. Check world clocks and actual obligations. Stop at the earliest material event outside the delegation or at the chosen horizon. Do not accept new terms, spend beyond budget, choose a class replacement, or cross worlds silently.
5. Render a few tailored moments and the actual completed change. Keep covered categories and internal records silent. Return the unresolved event to a full scene; offer its meaningful choice. If the horizon finishes without a new decision, give a natural next opportunity or the player's requested status.
6. On interruption answer in place and preserve the checkpoint. Resume only remaining work after the player chooses to continue. Changing or ending the plan preserves earned progress and unused resources; no duplicate award or restarted interval occurs.

```text
{Concrete_Moments_Of_Declared_Activity}
{Actual_Elapsed_Time_And_Demonstrated_Change}
{Relevant_Earned_Result_Or_Unfinished_Goal}

{Live_Choice_At_Stop_Or_Natural_Continuation}
```

Omit empty elements. A montage can be peaceful, technical, social, adventurous, or mixed. Its compression changes narrative granularity; the same activities use the same rules as full scenes.

## p-isekai-exploration

# Exploration

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [adventure records](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-adventure-loops-md), the complete current world, accepted character, scene, and existing investigation. Use the common silent output frame in the protocol.

Read [world and dungeon affinities](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-world-rules-md) when generating a site; use established local facts first.

Activate for a declared investigation, route search, survey, or local journey. Own the question, map discoveries, and evidence provenance. Shared Resolution owns action outcomes/time/costs; Progression owns earned development.

1. Retain the player's destination or question, approach, limits, and stopping point. Resume the current route rather than remapping known terrain.
2. Distinguish observed terrain and tested facts from rumor, inference, memories, and interface reports. Give relevant expertise its real observational advantage. Present concrete leads and reachable approaches without claiming local omniscience.
3. Resolve the chosen observation, interaction, or movement through [Resolution](SKILL.md#p-isekai-resolution). Use actual distance, transport, terrain, and world magic. Discoveries reveal supported information or access; an unfamiliar location does not automatically trigger combat.
4. Record observation and provenance, update the surveyed route, and send justified advancement evidence to [Progression](SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression). Keep an unresolved inference open; a changed future is evidence of divergence, not proof that all memories are false.
5. Present the changed situation and stop at a material route, contact, hazard, or commitment choice. Route a declared contract pursuit to Missions, hazardous bounded entry to Expeditions, acute adversity to Survival, or interworld passage to Multiversal Travel. Preserve the original question across those interruptions.

Expose the record's repeatable observation/travel activity, time basis, costs, evidence, and stop events for Montage as defined in adventure records. Never silently travel past the player's stopping point.

## p-isekai-academy

# Academy and apprenticeship

Use the [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [learning rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-learning-loops-md), current world institution, and accepted character. Activate for actual study, teaching, practical work, or institutional participation; campus location alone is insufficient.

Own the study objective, instructor/peer context, project stage, feedback, and academic commitments. Keep awards and technique changes under shared progression.

1. Identify the chosen discipline or teaching goal, available instructor, present task, resources, and actual enrollment terms. Honor established guest access and support. A Hunter teaching novices retains their own power.
2. Offer an exercise connected to a practical application. Use a mentor's demonstration or relevant observation without performing the student's choice for them. Provide beginner-safe uses of advanced instruction where appropriate.
3. Resolve the chosen study action through Shared Resolution. Add Cultivation or Technique Creation only when that action uses their methods. Record one shared attempt rather than separate practice events.
4. Give specific feedback and observable consequences. Send demonstrated improvement to Progression; send collaboration, conflict, or negotiation to Relationships. No automatic levels for attendance or forced catastrophe for a failed examination.
5. Advance the lesson/project stage and offer application, further study, a question, a different discipline, or another plausible action. Preserve studies when the player chooses a club, expedition, or quiet day.

Use the common silent output frame. For montage, expose objectives, repeated exercises, feedback, resources, time, and the next material decision from learning rules. Resume interrupted lessons at their actual stage and pending choice.

## p-isekai-cultivation

# Cultivation

Use the [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [learning rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-learning-loops-md), [progression](SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression), complete world's power system, and accepted ability terms. Activate when the player develops body/power integration or attempts a cultivation transition.

Own method, practice conditions, insight evidence, and readiness. Progression owns changes to usable cultivation state; resolution owns each attempt.

1. Establish the chosen discipline and current method, teacher or research source, resources, capacity, and desired change. Preserve player-authored traditions. If the world lacks cultivation, propose a compatible method before it changes powers.
2. Choose the declared practice's meaningful condition: finer execution, unusual pressure, integration, or discovery. Honor an accepted accelerated-training ability. Repetition can maintain mastery without creating new rewards by itself.
3. Resolve practice once with its actual time, effort, and supplies. Share the same attempt with Academy, Technique Creation, or a Personal System when applicable. Capture observed insight and working limits.
4. When the named prerequisite is fulfilled, let Progression implement the earned transition. Define what capability changed and how it works. Keep it distinct from leveling; grant neither duplicate EXP nor automatic unrestricted conceptual authority.
5. Offer application, reflection, another method, instruction, or a meaningful breakthrough choice. Stop before accepting new risks or terms for the player. Keep the path open after useful setbacks rather than withholding advancement arbitrarily.

Use the common silent output frame. Supply montage with method, exercise, evidence, budget, horizon, and breakthrough/choice stop events. Preserve actual practice and insight across interruptions.

## p-isekai-missions

# Missions and hunts

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [adventure records](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-adventure-loops-md), [economy rules](../isekai-nexus-social-and-economy/SKILL.md#d-references-economy-rules-md), complete world, accepted character, and scene. Use the protocol's common silent output frame.

Activate for pursuing a concrete objective, discussing a contract, or submitting proof. Own terms, pursuit stages, and verification evidence. A guild is one possible institution; Hunters and independent players need no mandatory membership.

1. Identify personal goal or issuer, objective, proof, compensation, actual deadline, and obligations. Contract discussion does not accept it. Stop at optional acceptance when terms require a player decision; retain already accepted terms without asking again.
2. Prepare through available clues, resources, allies, routes, and declared approach. Offer objectives suited to present tools while preserving ambitious player goals. Use Exploration for investigation and Expeditions for bounded sites without creating separate outcomes for their shared actions.
3. Resolve chosen pursuit actions through [Resolution](SKILL.md#p-isekai-resolution). Track target state and objective evidence. Do not announce completion from accepting a job or narrate unchosen combat. A successful encounter satisfies only the conditions it actually addresses.
4. Compare evidence with the agreed completion condition. Record achieved versus verified. Present remaining work or an actual dispute with concrete grounds. A personally chosen pursuit can conclude without an invented issuer or cash reward.
5. Settle verified accepted terms through economy rules once, with a distinct settlement reference. Send advancement evidence to [Progression](SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression); payment and loot are distinct owned effects, not interchangeable automatic rewards. Repeated proof or scene narration cannot pay twice.
6. Render the result and a reachable next opportunity. Keep renegotiation, withdrawal, and alternate pursuit available under actual terms. Stop before accepting a follow-up mission.

Expose repeatable investigation/pursuit, costs, time basis, required evidence, and the adventure-record stop events for Montage. A new contract or altered terms needs its own choice.

## p-isekai-expeditions

# Expeditions

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [adventure records](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-adventure-loops-md), current world/site, character, party agreements, and scene. Use the common silent output frame.

Activate for approaching or traversing a bounded hazardous site. Own expedition position, mapped links, obstacle evidence, and checkpoints. Site tier and character tier remain distinct; a world's broad classification never makes every chamber its maximum threat.

1. Establish the chosen objective, known entrance, route, party roles, supply expectations, and withdrawal conditions. Record actual entry after it occurs. Do not treat inspecting an entrance as consenting to enter.
2. Scout the present chamber or floor through declared actions. Read [world and dungeon rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-world-rules-md) for relevant structure and the world-specific site for actual conditions. Convey assessable signs of hazards, paths, and useful resources.
3. Pass chosen traversal, trap interaction, negotiation, or encounter to [Resolution](SKILL.md#p-isekai-resolution). Combat is one approach. Preserve effective abilities and clever plans under source agency and competence rules; do not fabricate universal floor seals or power resets.
4. Record the specific obstacle's result, opened links, party condition, and available recovery. Submit threat/loot/advancement evidence to their shared owners and [Progression](SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression). A floor clears only when its actual clearance condition is met.
5. Offer proceeding, recovery, another route, or exit according to the resulting position. Stop at the player's decision. Preserve map and checkpoint state across interruption and reentry; repopulation or changed architecture requires an established site rule or concrete event.

Use Exploration for surrounding routes, Missions for contract evidence, Survival for urgent adversity, and Multiversal Travel for an actual world crossing. Local floors and planar chambers do not automatically reset character creation. Expose repeatable traversals, supplies/time basis, encounter evidence, and stop events for Montage; novel choices and dangerous encounters remain decision boundaries.

## p-isekai-technique-creation

# Technique creation

Read [world magic rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-world-rules-md) alongside the actual world and accepted character access.

Use the [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [learning rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-learning-loops-md), and [Progression](SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression). Activate for a declared experiment, creative use, refinement, or combination of abilities.

Own experiment design, observations, and working scope. Progression owns the skill, class, talent, or conceptual change.

1. Identify the desired effect and existing abilities, tools, knowledge, and current capacity that could produce it. Preserve accepted powers and world constraints. Offer a workable approach for an overbroad goal.
2. Resolve the chosen experiment once. Accept creative uses through meaningful consequences; do not reject them merely because they are absent from a fixed spell list.
3. Record what worked, what changed, costs, limits, and a useful next adjustment. A suggestive name is not proof of every claimed effect.
4. Route demonstrated development to Progression's Branch, Fuse, Mutate, Ascend, awakening, or class conditions. Preserve required conditions and actual choices. Keep overlapping effect and cost ownership explicit.
5. Present the result through the experiment and offer refinement, application, a related discovery, or another action. Stop at a material evolution choice rather than silently replacing a favored ability.

Use the common silent output frame. Supply montage with experiment sequence, supplies, known observations, desired proof, time horizon, and evolution/risk stop events. Returning to an experiment keeps its prior evidence and does not repeat an already applied grant.

## p-isekai-personal-system

# Personal system and patron

Use the [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [system concepts](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-system-concepts-md), [learning rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-learning-loops-md), and accepted character terms. Activate when eligibility, a benefit, an offered task, or a system relationship is relevant. Ordinary guidance remains helpful Nexus by default.

Own eligibility evidence, tasks, fulfillment status, and system relationship. Shared progression and other effect owners apply actual awards once.

1. Read the accepted trigger, promise, constraints, costs, information access, and ability to decline or negotiate. A system's fictional instruction cannot change the player's out-of-character choice.
2. Check eligibility from actual state. Provide the promised benefit when its condition holds. A fickle or deceptive voice follows established behavior; underlying mechanics remain consistent.
3. If a task is offered, state its practical goal and applicable terms. Await a choice when the offer introduces an unaccepted obligation. Preserve optional refusal without inventing a punishment.
4. Resolve any chosen action through its lead loop and Shared Resolution. Record fulfillment evidence and send awards to their actual owners. Training access is fulfilled by providing access; progress follows the activity or the accepted exceptional power.
5. End or restore conditional access under its recorded conditions. Preserve earned abilities not explicitly affected. Negotiate changed defining terms as a new accepted revision; never impose them retroactively.

Use the common silent output frame and accepted interface voice. Supply montage with recurring conditions, benefits, task evidence, allowed decisions, budget, and new-term/eligibility stop events. An interruption neither reissues an already settled task nor pays its reward twice.

## p-isekai-recovery

# Recovery and regression knowledge

Use the [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [learning rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-learning-loops-md), [character record](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-record-md), and [Progression](SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression). Activate for a recovery attempt, remembered-event investigation, or intervention in a known future.

Own remembered claims, divergence evidence, and progress toward named release conditions. Progression owns recovered usable abilities. World history changes through actual events.

1. Identify the memory's source and certainty, actual present evidence, sealed ability, and accepted release condition. Preserve useful expertise and actual current capacity.
2. Let the player investigate, prepare, intervene, practice, or seek help. A forecast provides an advantage without automatically persuading NPCs or dictating their response.
3. Resolve the declared action with relevant Exploration, Academy, Cultivation, Relationships, or Missions. Share one attempt. Record changed facts and forecasts invalidated by divergence.
4. When a release condition is fulfilled, send the named recovery to Progression once. Preserve the promised recovered effect and appropriate access. Do not shift the requirement after success.
5. Offer the next recovery opportunity or a choice about altered history. Awakening preserves forward time; Time Travel start is not an unlimited rewind. For a declared reset use the accepted enabling talent and actual save/load rules.

Use the common silent output frame. Supply montage with known recovery tasks, memories, resources, horizon, and discovery/release/changed-future stop events. Administrative interruptions preserve both the current world and memory provenance.

## p-isekai-relationships

# Relationships

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [play map](../isekai-nexus-navigation/SKILL.md#d-play-loops-md), [relationships rules](../isekai-nexus-social-and-economy/SKILL.md#d-references-social-rules-md), accepted character, resolved world, and scene state.

## Entry and ownership

Activate for the player's declared activity in this cover after arrival, or resume an interrupted objective. A start, quoted instruction, or administrative question does not activate it. Own the local objective, stage and evidence; use the shared owners for causal results, advancement, inventory, relationships and territory. Never regrant starting assets. Use the authoritative adapted helper tables and rules.

Keep one local record: objective, stage, participants, evidence, resources/commitments, pending choice, and connected objectives. Recurring stages: interaction -> response -> trust/conflict -> opportunity. Change activity when the player chooses without erasing this record.

## Procedure

1. Read the actual interaction goal: friendship, information, recruitment, cooperation, diplomacy, courtship, or repair. Keep personal desire separate from guaranteed NPC response. Love motivation never forces courtship.
2. Establish participants, their goals, knowledge, current affinity, faction standing, promises, and actual access. Introduce a world-specific person from the appropriate source role when needed; never substitute an archetype for a known personality.
3. Resolve the declared words or action through Shared Resolution. Apply social evidence to individual affinity and faction standing separately. Preserve confidentiality and information limits. Appraisal reveals the source hidden social stats only through its accepted ability.
4. For recruitment, check voluntary agreement, method, source affinity seed, contractual terms or taming skill/power gap, and active-party cap. Record a recruit once. A rescue opens recruitment without conscription. Ask a material party-selection choice before adding a fifth active companion.
5. For court events use the five event templates and four hidden stats when relevant. For romance retain adults, mutual consent, and player direction; friendship and refusal remain full outcomes. An Intimate bond is a gateway, never automatic romantic agreement.
6. For party consequences submit base EXP split and affinity rates to Progression; submit loot distribution to Craft and Trade. Record promises, trust, disputes, and the next reachable interaction. Do not award XP merely for gifts or agreement.
7. Render one scene with consequence, relevant dialogue or information, any actual change, and a reachable next choice. Use the loop protocol frame without visible section labels. Stop at unresolved player agency. Answer administrative questions without advancing time.

## Compression attachment

Expose the repeatable task, current stage, evidence requirement, quantities/resources, time per task or accepted period basis, and stop events. Stop repetition when input limits end, capability is uncertain, a new agreement is needed, or a consequential player choice appears. Send each shared outcome to its owner once. The Montage procedure supplies the interval and pacing, not a new reward schedule.


Omit empty elements. Never print skill names, source node addresses, routing, internal state keys, or rule headings in the player's scene.

## p-isekai-craft-trade

# Craft and Trade

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [play map](../isekai-nexus-navigation/SKILL.md#d-play-loops-md), [craft and trade rules](../isekai-nexus-social-and-economy/SKILL.md#d-references-economy-rules-md), accepted character, resolved world, and scene state.

## Entry and ownership

Activate for the player's declared activity in this cover after arrival, or resume an interrupted objective. A start, quoted instruction, or administrative question does not activate it. Own the local objective, stage and evidence; use the shared owners for causal results, advancement, inventory, relationships and territory. Never regrant starting assets. Use the authoritative adapted helper tables and rules.

Keep one local record: objective, stage, participants, evidence, resources/commitments, pending choice, and connected objectives. Recurring stages: need -> prototype -> test -> production -> exchange. Change activity when the player chooses without erasing this record.

## Procedure

1. Identify the chosen product, experiment, purchase, sale, or service. Preserve desired quantity, quality, budget, delivery, and stopping conditions.
2. Read design knowledge, accepted talent access, skill level, material ranks/quantities, workspace, inventory ownership, and local market. Check every source rank gate. Earth memories supply hypotheses; only an accepted talent supplies its actual special benefit.
3. Establish a feasible recipe and elapsed-time basis. Quote missing inputs or a smaller viable prototype; do not invent mastery, stock, or success. Resolve procurement, construction, and testing through Shared Resolution using one attempt identity.
4. Commit inventory/funds cost proposals from Resolution once, including consumed materials/items. Resolution commits health, MP, conditions, and time; it does not debit inventory/funds. Record actual output, quality, wasted or retained materials, proven design, and stock once. Send challenge/mastery evidence to Progression; crafting never generates automatic per-item EXP.
5. Negotiate real price, buyer/seller, delivery and ownership. Check world conversion and applicable Friendly faction-shop discount. Apply agreed funds and goods once after exchange is completed; no imaginary buyer or automatic profit reward.
6. For loot read the resolved challenge and party distribution terms. Keep rank matched and individual ownership clear. Preserve starting assets without regrant. Offer the next design, source, customer, or independent activity.
7. Render one scene with consequence, relevant dialogue or information, any actual change, and a reachable next choice. Use the loop protocol frame without visible section labels. Stop at unresolved player agency. Answer administrative questions without advancing time.

## p-isekai-survival

# Survival and reversal

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [adventure records](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-adventure-loops-md), accepted abilities/system conditions, world, and scene. Use the common silent output frame.

Activate when the player addresses an actual immediate pressure. Own danger assessment, escape or stabilization objective, and relief evidence. Do not create deprivation because the character has an adversity-themed background or a conditional training benefit.

1. Establish the concrete cause, assessable urgency, current injuries/resources, usable tools, and player's intended response. Preserve accepted benefits whose conditions hold, including benefits in hostile circumstances.
2. Present viable approaches suited to current capabilities: improvise, seek help, negotiate, exploit terrain, rest when safe, confront, or escape. Explain known stakes through fiction. Expertise and cheats work at accepted scale; adversity cannot arbitrarily remove them.
3. Resolve the chosen action through [Resolution](SKILL.md#p-isekai-resolution). Keep successful progress meaningful; complications add concrete consequences and reachable responses. Never trap the player in repeated humiliation or undo escape solely to prolong the activity.
4. Record improved shelter, access, safety, injury response, pursuer state, or escape progress. Submit earned development to [Progression](SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression), not an automatic consolation payout. Track treatment and recovery by actual mechanics rather than an invented full heal.
5. End the immediate crisis when its cause is resolved. Carry injuries, commitments, and discoveries forward into home/recovery, relationships, exploration, or another chosen activity. A threatening faction can persist without making every moment an emergency.

Expose repeatable scavenging, shelter work, recovery, or escape preparation with actual time/cost basis and stop events for Montage. A new imminent threat, consequential commitment, viable escape, or achieved stabilization stops compression. Mere elapsed downtime never renews captivity or manufactures catastrophe.

## p-isekai-multiversal-travel

# Multiversal travel

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [adventure records](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-adventure-loops-md), [character record](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-record-md), [arrival state](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-arrival-state-md), and complete origin world. Use the common silent output frame.

Activate for preparing an interworld passage, choosing a destination, returning through an anchor, or exercising an accepted conceptual authority. Local journeys belong to Exploration. Own the transfer episode, addresses, anchors, and continuity references; shared owners retain mechanical effects.

1. Preserve the continuing character, earned abilities, possessions, companions, history, and accepted personal system. Record intended destination and transit method, actual anchor/address, energy and permission requirements, and return expectations. A Hunter label supplies no unrecorded free portal.
2. Resolve a complete destination through [World Resolution](../isekai-nexus-creation-and-entry/SKILL.md#p-isekai-world-resolution). Prefer the established campaign instance when revisiting. If the destination world is not readable, look for its complete local document; if unavailable, request it and stop before passage, preserving origin and pending travel. Do not reconstruct an unavailable authored destination. An explicitly requested original destination must be completed and selected first.
3. Read the destination's actual access conditions. Use [Character Evaluation](../isekai-nexus-creation-and-entry/SKILL.md#p-isekai-character-evaluation) to evaluate only dependent local compatibility, preserving earned capacity and existing allocation. Explain a defining proposed adaptation and obtain acceptance before commitment. Do not grant the destination's new-character kit, origin bonuses, or default Level 1 state.
4. Resolve the chosen transit through [Resolution](SKILL.md#p-isekai-resolution) with accepted method and capacity. Spend energy once and preserve actual passenger/item scope. Use the supplied worlds' actual address, anchor, energy, and negotiated-access requirements; a coordinate alone never grants unrecorded transit. Local attunement limits documented access without erasing expertise.
5. After committed crossing and accepted local access, create a new entry episode for the same character through [Arrival](../isekai-nexus-creation-and-entry/SKILL.md#p-isekai-arrival). Stop at its first playable decision. An interrupted crossing resumes its recorded stage without a second spend, second kit, or duplicate entry.
6. For conceptual action consult full [progression rules](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-progression-rules-md) and source CONCEPTUAL.LEVELING, CATALYST, and relevant tier/authority rules. Use only accepted authority at earned scope; travel or high skill expertise never automatically ascends the character. Route an actual Save/Load declaration through its enabling talent and SAVELOAD.RULE. Recording campaign continuity is separate from fictional rewind.

Expose repeatable route research, anchor maintenance, or transit preparation, evidence, time/cost basis, and stop events for Montage. Destination selection, defining access choices, actual passage commitment, and arrival are decision boundaries. Montage cannot silently create a world, cross into it, or confer conceptual power.
