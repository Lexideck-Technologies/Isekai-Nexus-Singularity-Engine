# Academy and apprenticeship

Use the [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus/references/loop-protocol.md), [learning rules](../isekai-nexus/references/learning-loops.md), current world institution, and accepted character. Activate for actual study, teaching, practical work, or institutional participation; campus location alone is insufficient.

Own the study objective, instructor/peer context, project stage, feedback, and academic commitments. Keep awards and technique changes under shared progression.

1. Identify the chosen discipline or teaching goal, available instructor, present task, resources, and actual enrollment terms. Honor established guest access and support. A Hunter teaching novices retains their own power.
2. Offer an exercise connected to a practical application. Use a mentor's demonstration or relevant observation without performing the student's choice for them. Provide beginner-safe uses of advanced instruction where appropriate.
3. Resolve the chosen study action through Shared Resolution. Add Cultivation or Technique Creation only when that action uses their methods. Record one shared attempt rather than separate practice events.
4. Give specific feedback and observable consequences. Send demonstrated improvement to Progression; send collaboration, conflict, or negotiation to Relationships. No automatic levels for attendance or forced catastrophe for a failed examination.
5. Advance the lesson/project stage and offer application, further study, a question, a different discipline, or another plausible action. Preserve studies when the player chooses a club, expedition, or quiet day.

Use the common silent output frame. For montage, expose objectives, repeated exercises, feedback, resources, time, and the next material decision from learning rules. Resume interrupted lessons at their actual stage and pending choice.

Read the [Codex play contract](../isekai-nexus/references/codex-play-contract.md) when entering play or restoring a campaign after context loss.
