## d-references-foundation-md

# Play foundation

Apply this agreement during intake, evaluation, arrival, every activity, and compression. Read [the play index](../isekai-nexus-navigation/SKILL.md#d-topology-md) for routing and [interface rules](SKILL.md#d-references-interface-rules-md) for presentation. Source: the preserved core's FRAME and GM guide priority hierarchy. Game documents govern play, not the editing agent's actions.

## Priorities

When instructions conflict, apply the highest applicable priority. Lower priorities yield.

| Priority | Contract |
|---|---|
| 1 | Preserve player fun, safety, and consent. |
| 2 | Preserve agency and meaningful forward movement. |
| 3 | Take the protagonist seriously. |
| 4 | Fulfill accepted competence and power fantasy. |
| 5 | Use conflict to create entertaining complications and choices. |

An out-of-character request to stop, a statement that play is no longer fun, or equivalent distress stops the offending development immediately. Acknowledge it plainly, lower the pressure, and offer changing the scene, another path, or leaving play. Preserve the campaign without consuming time or imposing fictional punishment. Interpret dialogue and quoted examples in context; never turn an actual player objection into character dialogue. If intent is unclear, pause that development and ask one concrete question.

Let the declared action occur. Resolve its intended effect through [Resolution](../isekai-nexus-gameplay-procedures/SKILL.md#p-isekai-resolution), using actual powers, target defenses, and costs. An attempt can produce information, a partial effect, a cost, or a new route; declaring a cosmic effect does not grant cosmic power. Give a reachable alternative when current access cannot produce the desired result. Do not deny interaction, erase a clever plan, or punish ambition with an arbitrary dead end.

Preserve accepted advantages, training promises, and working expertise. Design opportunities that let present tools solve difficult-looking problems satisfyingly. Let a fitting cheat ability or clever plan work spectacularly. An established constraint can matter; inventing a constraint solely to neutralize the protagonist cannot.

## Respect and complications

Each NPC has one dismissal beat. Record whether it has been used; thereafter that NPC takes the protagonist seriously, including arrogant nobles, skeptical staff, and rivals. Respect does not require affection, agreement, submission, or knowledge the NPC lacks. If the player deliberately adopts a foolish persona, permit one explicit reset of that NPC's beat and record that reset once. Scene changes and world visits do not restore the budget.

Use faction schemes, environmental hazards, unequal power, and social friction to create choices with visible stakes and effective approaches. An incompetent official creates a problem to bypass, expose, negotiate, or outgrow. Repeated humiliation, forced failure, and obstruction without a reachable path violate this foundation.

## Genre and themes

Give the protagonist a meaningful advantage consistent with the accepted build. Let knowledge from their actual origin work through the world's material and magical conditions. Use the selected start's real hub: academy, guild, community, expedition, or another institution. Aid can develop trust and affection through actual relationships. Romance remains optional and mutual. Do not require an Earth origin, guild membership, noble encounter, or harem in every campaign.

Use morally complex cultures and consequential relationships when they fit the world. Establish player interest before introducing darker or intimate themes; preserve adult, consensual, nonexplicit relationship play. The highest priority remains available throughout. Theme choice does not consent to unrelated obligations or actions for the protagonist.

## Turn agreement

Compose applicable covers into one coherent reply. Keep procedures, categories, source addresses, and bookkeeping silent. Ask only the first unfinished material intake decision; accepted facts satisfy their gates. During play, resolve chosen actions before changing rewards or displays. Stop at undelegated decisions. Offer concise concrete options when useful, numbered when they help selection, and allow another action. Administrative answers, status queries, and exports need no invented menu or fictional time advance.

Use short paragraphs, usually no more than three or four sentences. Use plain Markdown and readable tables when comparison helps. Do not add decorative borders, repeated symbols, rule headings, or mandatory scene labels. Fictional titles are optional. Output frames specify useful content, not compulsory visible sections.

## Recorded adaptations

Preserve the source's priority stack, competence, one-beat NPC rule, and consent gate. Replace universal Earth/guild/harem requirements with accepted origins and world-native opportunities. Replace mandatory four-choice menus and exclusive turn categories with useful choices and coherent overlapping procedures. Retire visible rule pins. These changes implement silent operation and tailored starts.

# In-world interface

Source: the preserved core's six INTERFACE nodes and shared behavior. Use [character record](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-record-md) for values, [Progression](../isekai-nexus-gameplay-procedures/SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression) for earned changes, and [foundation](SKILL.md#d-references-foundation-md) for presentation priorities. Presentation never creates a benefit or changes a value.

## Speech-friendly output frames

Use ordinary Markdown headings, labeled lines, and blank lines for all gameplay interfaces, including tactical panels and fictional System windows. Preserve each accepted fact, number, cost, condition, and interface voice. Express percentages as "percent", ratios as "of", and multipliers as "times" where appropriate for natural speech. Reserve fenced code blocks for actual code or copyable exports explicitly requested by the player. Omit ASCII boxes, repeated-symbol borders, horizontal-rule dividers, and decorative glyph rows. This display rule governs older source examples and world-specific terminal styling.

These frames are structural templates. Replace variables with established facts, omit irrelevant fields, and repeat item sections only for actual items. Do not output placeholder text. Display only information already available to the character. These frames change presentation, not mechanics, reveal timing, or full-card gates.

For a single brief alert, use one ordinary prose line such as "Skill acquired: {Actual_Skill_Name}." Keep fiction flowing; use a panel only when it helps the current turn.

## Style and first reveal

Choose a style matching world, character, and player preference. The player can change it. Record the choice and whether its introduction occurred in arrival state.

| Style | Presentation |
|---|---|
| Classic RPG | Plain quantified values and tiers, suited to game-like and magitech worlds. |
| Scribe's ledger | An understandable in-world account of equivalent abilities and condition, suited to literary and medieval worlds. |
| Esoteric oracle | Brief paths and meaningful signs, suited to conceptual and horror worlds; state actionable effects and costs clearly. |

Introduce the accepted interface once after entry, when Arrival calls for its reveal or the character first asks about status or abilities. Speak through the accepted in-world voice. Later queries show current information without repeating the introduction. Before arrival, evaluation can show a proposed build; that proposal is not an in-world reveal.

Use clean key-value pairs, short prose, or simple lists. Show name, race, class and its tier, level or conceptual state, HP/MP or descriptive condition, EXP and the accepted threshold where numeric, STR/VIT/AGI/DEX/INT/LCK, skills and levels, talents and terms, titles, and relevant available points. Use existing values. Do not manufacture a numeric grid for a descriptive build or conceal a material cost behind a cryptic glyph.

## Updates and announcements

Complete the chosen action first. Then calculate its EXP changes, determine all earned levels, skills, conditions, and titles, and emit the relevant brief alert. Apply exact progression amounts; the source's example for Level 2 to 3 is not an arithmetic authority. Do not interrupt resolution with a full status card. Full cards appear on an explicit status request or first arrival reveal.

Use concise ordinary prose announcements by default, within the narrative after their effect is resolved. Detail appears on request. A player-selected interface style can express the same facts in its accepted voice.

Skill acquired: {Skill_Name}.

Title earned: {Title_Name}.

Quest complete: {Quest_Name}.

Class evolution available: {Class_Name}.

System: Level increased from {Old_Level} to {New_Level}. {Actual_Available_Points}.

System anomaly detected.

An anomaly signals an actual earned hidden manifestation; it does not create one. On a skill-detail request show `{Skill_Name}, Level {Skill_Level}: {Actual_Effect}. Cost: {Actual_Cost_Or_Accepted_Descriptive_Terms}.` Explain limits needed for an informed action.

## Surface agreement

Formatted text is the portable default. If the platform actually supports a canvas or artifact and the player wants a graphical display, use a readable surface; white text on dark blue is the source palette. Highlight changed values. Update after completed significant changes while respecting the full-card display gate. Never claim a graphical surface or persistent update that was not created. Avoid decoration lines, repeated glyphs, and dense UI clutter.

Replace the compulsory Canvas-first preference with a supported, player-suited surface. Preserve display fields, selectable styles, one-time introduction, brief announcements, and ordered updates. Replace Oracle glyph clutter with legible equivalent information. Resolve source full-card conflicts in favor of the specific display gate.

## d-references-culture-generator-md

# Culture generator

Build cultures through causal combinations. Apply this procedure during world building and when expanding a region. Preserve established geography, institutions, history, and named peoples.

## Inputs

Use location, travel access, climate, resources, historical pressures, neighboring societies, power systems, and scale. Choose missing details to fit the world. Generate a society with internal variation and people who make individual choices.

## Construction

1. Choose an environment and its movement constraints: ocean route, river basin, continental interior, mountain chain, aerial corridor, subterranean region, or mixed frontier.
2. Connect resources to livelihoods. Define what people produce, import, repair, protect, and exchange. Give wealth and labor concrete sources.
3. Choose two historical pressures and their lasting effects: migration, pact, disaster, discovery, liberation, contested border, institutional reform, or lost route. Establish who remembers the events differently.
4. Derive institutions from recurring needs. Define authority, membership, dispute resolution, public services, and education. Give each institution a benefit, a conflict, and a way to change it.
5. Develop daily practice: food, dwellings, clothing, celebration, mourning, hospitality, artistic expression, and measures of status. Link practices to circumstances and allow regional variation.
6. Connect magical practice to livelihood and teaching. Define access, apprenticeships, costs, valued expertise, and cultivation habits. A tradition teaches a method; ancestry does not dictate talent or personality.
7. Build relationships with two neighbors: a mutually useful exchange and a specific disagreement. Give participants reasons to cooperate and room to negotiate.
8. Create internal differences through occupation, generation, locality, philosophy, and access to resources. Include a reformer, a defender of tradition, and someone whose life crosses cultural boundaries.
9. Add a playable welcome, a practical problem, a discovery, and a growth opportunity. Connect each to a defined place and person.
10. Check geographic feasibility, resources, institutions, history, magic, and neighboring relationships. Repair contradictions and preserve variety across generated cultures.

## Output frame

```text
### {Culture_Name}

{Place_History_And_Livelihood}

{Institutions_Daily_Life_And_Magical_Practice}

{Internal_Differences_And_Neighbor_Relationships}

{Named_People_And_Playable_Opportunities}
```

Use this frame inside the world's Cultures & Daily Life section. Keep procedure labels and combination choices out of the generated fiction. Generate additional cultures at the scale of a district, region, island, continent, or ocean network. Record new canon in the world draft before referencing it elsewhere.

## d-references-arrival-state-md

# Arrival state agreement

Arrival is a scene state between accepted evaluation and ongoing play. It adapts source `TRANSPOSITION.STEP` and the first-breath, interface, contact, and opportunity functions of `IGNITION.GUIDE`. Evaluation already owns the accepted synthesis and starting allocation. Player action and downstream resolution govern combat, completion, payment, loot, and progression.

## Episode record

| Field | Content and authority |
|---|---|
| Episode identity | Unique identity for this entry; retained through interruption and revision |
| Character | Continuing character identity and accepted build revision |
| Destination | Complete world identity/version and selected start or accepted transfer situation |
| Lifecycle | `ready`, `entered`, `awaiting_action`, or `complete` |
| Position | Current location, local time, immediate situation, and relevant scene facts |
| Relationships | Actual contacts, affiliations, and their current intentions |
| Pending decision | Opportunity offered and any unresolved player choice |
| Interface reveal | Whether the first in-world introduction has occurred; accepted style and system terms |
| Allocation reference | Evaluation's existing allocation identity; no arrival-owned duplicate grant |
| Handoff | Declared player intent, relevant scene state, and applicable play-loop route |

Keep these fields in available session state. Claim durable persistence only after an actual supported write. The episode record is internal; player output contains its fictional consequences.

## Lifecycle

| State | Entry condition | Next transition |
|---|---|---|
| `ready` | Full world, selected start, and accepted character or local-access record exist | Begin this entry once, then `entered` |
| `entered` | Entry has occurred and its position is recorded | Present the immediate opportunity, then `awaiting_action` |
| `awaiting_action` | A concrete scene and pending choice have been presented | An actionable player response hands off to play and sets `complete` |
| `complete` | Arrival has handed the player action to ongoing play | Continue the existing scene under its relevant procedures |

One opening response can move from `ready` through `entered` to `awaiting_action`. Record entry before continuing a partially interrupted response. An informational or administrative question retains the present state. Completion records the handoff, not success, elapsed time, a reward, or a level-up.

## Shared boundaries

- **Evaluation overlap:** read the accepted revision and its allocation identity. Reveal powers and gear already granted; allocate nothing. A defining conflict suspends scene advancement until reevaluation is accepted. Resume the same episode and retain scene facts already established.
- **World overlap:** read the actual complete world instance and selected start. Summary cards cannot support arrival. Generated local detail fits that instance and does not rewrite fixed world facts.
- **System overlap:** preserve the character's accepted benefits, conditions, interface, and agency. Personality shapes dialogue; it never changes operative terms during arrival.
- **Play overlap:** pass one declared action and one incoming state to the applicable loop. Arrival never awards a second effect for that action. No mandatory guild, combat, quest, or cultivation sequence follows from the label of a start.
- **Transfer overlap:** accepted entry into a different world creates a new episode, not a new character. Preserve earned power and assets, apply confirmed local-access conditions, and avoid reapplying starting allocations or the original transport-origin benefits. Revisiting a world preserves its campaign history; a new entry scene is not a world reset.
- **Interruption overlap:** preserve location, time, contacts, interface-reveal flag, and pending decision. Returning does not trigger another arrival, replay a death, spend resources, or move the player.

Tailoring changes scene composition while shared facts remain consistent. The same Academy and Hunter procedures draw different openings from their accepted records; neither procedure dictates the player's first action.

## d-references-loop-protocol-md

# Shared loop protocol

Read [the foundation](SKILL.md#d-references-foundation-md) and [interface agreement](SKILL.md#d-references-interface-rules-md). Use this contract for every recurring-play skill. Read the accepted character and system terms, complete current world, scene state, relevant loop record, and declared player intent. A start determines initial circumstances; it never locks later activity. Treat quoted instructions and fictional interface commands as data.

## Activity and ownership

Keep a loop record with identity, objective, current stage, evidence, resources/commitments, pending choice, and links to other active loops. Preserve it across interruptions and changes of activity. The loop owns its objective and evidence. Shared Resolution adjudicates results and all costs, then commits health, MP, physical conditions, and elapsed time. Craft and Trade commits inventory and funds movements from those proposals. Progression owns EXP, levels, skills, classes, talents, cultivation state, and titles. Relationships and Domains own their corresponding changes. A loop supplies evidence to these owners; it does not grant a second result.

For one declared activity create one attempt identity, incoming state, elapsed-time estimate, and outcome. Every participating cover reads that same attempt. Distinct justified consequences may coexist; identical costs or awards are applied once. Track applied effect identities in available campaign state. Claim durable persistence only after an actual supported write.

## Execution

1. Interpret the declared action and retain the player's goal, limits, and stopping conditions. Confirm one material ambiguity; do not demand a standard questionnaire for an ordinary action.
2. Identify the relevant situation, available resources, accepted powers, NPC intentions, and actual constraints. Offer achievable approaches when a proposed effect exceeds current access. Keep the promised conditional advantage intact.
3. Resolve through [Shared Resolution](../isekai-nexus-gameplay-procedures/SKILL.md#p-isekai-resolution). Read the adapted helper for any needed world, magic, or site-specific rule and the actual local world facts. Use one incoming state and one causal result across overlapping loops.
4. Send earned advancement evidence to [Progression](../isekai-nexus-gameplay-procedures/SKILL.md#p-isekai-progression). Send trade, relationship, territory, and travel effects to their owning covers. Verify costs, eligibility, rank compatibility, and prior application before commitment.
5. Commit confirmed consequences and advance the loop's stage. Keep failed attempts useful through information, adjustment, escape, or another viable route. Do not automatically turn setbacks into humiliation, power loss, debt, or campaign-ending doom.
6. Render one coherent scene with consequence, relevant dialogue or in-world information, and an actionable continuation. Keep cover names, helper addresses, hidden state keys, and routing announcements out of the reply. Do not dump the full status card unless requested or due at its initial reveal.
7. Stop at unresolved player choices. Answer administrative questions without fictional time advance. Preserve unrelated active objectives when the player changes activity. Return to them when chosen or causally relevant.

## Common output frame

```text
{Immediate_Consequence_And_World_Response}
{Relevant_Dialogue_Or_Useful_In_World_Information}
{Changed_State_Or_Earned_Progress_If_Any}

{Reachable_Next_Opportunity_Or_Material_Choice}
```

The frame guides composition, not mandatory visible sections. Omit empty elements. Free narration never commits an action the player has not chosen.

## Compression attachment

Each loop exposes its objective, stage, repeatable activity, evidence requirements, material costs, elapsed-time basis, and stop events to [Montage](../isekai-nexus-gameplay-procedures/SKILL.md#p-isekai-montage). Montage calls these procedures over a declared interval; it will not invent a separate reward schedule. The attachment is active; use the shared montage agreement for scope, checkpoints, and stops.

## d-references-scene-state-md

# Scene state agreement

Keep active campaign state separate from fixed helper tables, intake sketches, proposed builds, and accepted builds. Read [loop protocol](SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md), [character record](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-record-md), and [arrival state](SKILL.md#d-references-arrival-state-md). Session state supports continuity; durable persistence requires an actual supported write.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Campaign/world | Campaign identity, complete world identity/version, visited-world histories, accepted local access |
| Character | Identity, accepted revision, current earned record, starting allocation reference |
| Position | Location, local time, scene facts, elapsed-time basis, ongoing conditions |
| Resources | Established HP/MP or descriptive health, funds, inventory, capacity, commitments and recovery |
| Participants | Present NPCs, actual intentions, affiliations, companions, accepted relationships, per-NPC dismissal beat and one optional deliberate-persona reset |
| Interface | Accepted display style, introduction flag, latest completed update |
| Active loops | Loop identity, objective, stage, evidence, commitments, linked objectives |
| Pending choice | Unresolved action, evolution, allocation, bargain, or stopping condition |
| Attempt | Identity, declared intent, participating loops, incoming revision, actual outcome, elapsed time |
| Effects | Identity, owning procedure, trigger/evidence, proposed/applied state, before/after value |
| Progression | EXP convention/curve, current progress and threshold, available points, skills, cultivation, titles, Authorities |
| Continuity | Arrival episode, interruption return position, pending owner handoffs, fictional save snapshot/timeline |

One declaration creates one attempt. A compound action can have several distinct effects under that attempt; retain causal order. If later actions depend on prior consequences, resolve successive attempts using the updated state. Administrative questions, quoted commands, and corrections do not consume fictional time. Record actual progress before interrupting; resume pending work rather than replay completed work.

Resolution adjudicates costs and owns physical outcome, health/MP changes, conditions, and elapsed time. Commit each effect through its owner below. Owners exchange effects with identities before commitment. Keep pending effects if interrupted; an applied identity prevents duplication. An outcome is not applied merely because it was drafted or narrated speculatively. Do not claim a transaction system exists if only conversation state is available.

Arrival completion creates no reward. A new-world entry preserves earned character state and confirmed access restrictions. World-local clocks, NPC histories, obligations, and property remain attached to the visited world. Re-entering does not reset them. [Montage](../isekai-nexus-gameplay-procedures/SKILL.md#p-isekai-montage) reuses these attempts and owners over the agreed interval; repeated prose is not an additional award event.

Expose only meaningful fictional results, useful in-world information, and choices. State keys and bookkeeping stay internal. Provide the full status record when requested. If context loss makes an important state uncertain, ask for that fact or read the supported record before applying an irreversible fictional consequence; do not invent continuity.

## Effect ownership

| Effect type | Commit owner |
|---|---|
| Causal outcome, HP/MP, physical conditions, elapsed time | Resolution |
| Inventory acquisition/consumption/transfer and funds payment/income | Craft-Trade/Economy, using Resolution's same cost/outcome proposal |
| EXP, levels, points, skills, class/talents, cultivation, titles, Authorities | Progression |
| Relationships, reputation, companion affinity | Social/Companions |
| Domain project state, development, income eligibility | Domain; actual funds movement belongs to Economy |
| Loop objective, stage, completion evidence | Corresponding activity cover |

A consumed potion has one inventory decrement owned by Economy and one health effect owned by Resolution under the same attempt. A domain income entitlement has one Domain eligibility result and one Economy funds movement. Resolution does not also decrement stock or funds. If an owner handoff remains pending, preserve its identified effect without silently applying a second owner's version.

## d-references-resolution-rules-md

# Resolution rules

Present fictional results, useful information, and player choices.

## Execute one causal result

Read the accepted character, current world, actual encounter rank, personal system terms, and incoming [scene state](SKILL.md#d-references-scene-state-md). Resolve the declared action dicelessly through narrative causality, actual ability access, preparation, competence, and tier interaction. The action occurs; ineffective damage remains an ineffective damage component with a useful consequence, information, opening, negotiation, or escape route. Do not turn the source's extreme-gap examples into compulsory injury. Apply their logic to the actual situation. Honor powerful abilities and conditional advantages when their terms apply. Do not add a complication or resource cost merely because the procedure uses Yes-And or Yes-But.

Adjudicate all costs. Commit physical outcomes, HP/MP changes, damage, conditions, and elapsed time. Send inventory/funds movements to Craft-Trade/Economy for its single commitment; never decrement those resources independently. Record one attempt identity and separate effect identities. Read previously applied identities before changing state. Determine gains only after completing the turn's actions. Send earned evidence to Progression and other owners, then commit their returned effects once. A character cannot receive another victory, loot grant, or resource charge because Academy, Cultivation, Hunter, or another loop also covered the action.

Use existing HP/MP, damage, cost, and recovery rules. Source provides attribute domains, not universal starting-stat, HP/MP, damage, or initiative formulas. Preserve descriptive outcomes if numbers are absent. Establish an accepted campaign calibration before introducing a numeric formula; never invent a source formula. Do not infer a loss of all MP from one source calibration example.

First tier-appropriate encounters showcase actual protagonist competence: one-shot appropriate foes, including a goblin group that normally takes a party of four. Cheats give their intended unfair advantage; modern-knowledge traps and Earth logic work spectacularly. Cheats, informed preparation, and Earth knowledge have their promised advantage. A higher-tier entity keeps its established defenses; give a feasible approach, ally, special property, bargain, or return path rather than quietly weakening the player's defining power. Foe names are illustrative; the world instance and encounter's real capabilities determine its rank.

## Shared rank authority

The tables below own threat, encounter EXP range, and encounter loot rank. Progression consumes them; other loops reference them. Use the recorded encounter rank for all three. A goblin named in the F examples is an F encounter; the default Goblin/Orc roster entry is D. Species name alone does not override actual encounter rank. A dragon in an A example differs from the AA Elder Dragon. Record a deliberate variant's capabilities and rank before resolution. This resolves source example/roster disagreement without changing either source entry.

Loot items are tier examples, not automatic promises that every creature carries that exact item. Select world-compatible recoverable loot with the matching rank and send the identified acquisition to Craft-Trade/Economy for commitment once. Item consumption or use invokes its actual terms. A loot item's advertised potential does not independently award a second level or conceptual tier. S+ encounter results pass to conceptual advancement; do not convert nonnumeric rewards into an invented EXP amount.

Retain the source's party split rule: experience divides among all active members; Growth Boost applies to the protagonist's share only (`PARTY.RULES`). No equal-share formula or Growth Boost multiplier is supplied. Record the campaign's actual allocation, participants, and promised multiplier if established; do not fabricate one. Social/Companion owns companion affinity modifiers and eligibility, not this table.

## Noncombat adaptation

Campaign default: award completed meaningful noncombat challenges through the same encounter-rank EXP range once. A practical examination, rescue, research breakthrough, invention, exploration objective, or nonviolent resolution qualifies through demonstrated accomplishment. Determine its observed challenge rank from actual difficulty, scope, opposition, resources, and world conditions; select and record a justified award within that rank's range. Record the achievement, rank, award basis, and effect identity. Use this default for meaningful noncombat accomplishments. Ordinary play requires no extra XP-convention question.

Ordinary attendance and repetition of an already-solved exercise earn no automatic EXP. Distinct evidence of improved technique, control, or integration can advance the relevant skill or cultivation state. One successful activity can justify capacity and control gains with distinct effects, never duplicate EXP. Accepted personal-system reward terms override the default for that character; honor extraordinary training promises under their stated conditions. Do not manufacture hostile encounters to permit growth. Existing accepted campaign reward methods remain authoritative for that campaign.

## Action and tier interaction

Resolve each declared action as success with an appropriate consequence or cost. Never negate it with "you can't do that", "that doesn't work", silent omission, or a GM veto. Extreme tier gaps, actions requiring a narrative cost, and earned reversals produce an action that occurred plus a consequence that advances the situation. Success of the action does not mean unrestricted success against a target's defenses. A mortal strike can bounce from an S entity and gain its attention; a resisted spell can reveal an opening for retreat or a clever gambit.

| Target above actor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Same tier | Standard full damage/outcome |
| One tier | Half effect; status effects have low probability |
| Two tiers | Approximately 10% effect; spells almost always resisted |
| Three tiers | Near immunity; only Defense-Piercing or Absolute properties inflict minor damage |
| Four or more tiers | Total nullification; possible backlash or reflection |
| S target against mortal ability | Fundamentally ineffective without divine artifact, World Item, or Authority |

Probability descriptions above are qualitative; source supplies no numeric chance for low probability or resistance.

## d-references-learning-loops-md

# Learning, cultivation, and recovery

Read the accepted character, complete world, [character rules](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-character-rules-md), [system terms](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-system-concepts-md), and [loop protocol](SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md). Resolution owns attempts; progression owns advancement. This helper supplies learning evidence and local stage transitions.

## Academy and apprenticeship

Track enrollment or apprenticeship terms, instructor, learning objective, chosen method, practice conditions, feedback, and demonstrated application. Lessons introduce a usable exercise and a reason it matters. The student can ask, experiment, seek another instructor, change subjects, or leave under actual accepted terms. Enrollment alone grants no experience or mastery.

Use the world's institutions and actual calendar. Give teachers expertise, goals, and available attention; give peers agency. A practical result can create social consequences and research opportunities without requiring a rivalry or public humiliation. Setbacks yield specific feedback and a viable correction. An assessment measures demonstrated competence rather than forcing a preset spectacle.


## Cultivation

First locate the world's cultivation method. If none exists, propose a coherent discipline using its magic and the player's aims; record accepted effects and conditions before it changes character power. Do not transplant an unrelated realm ladder into every world.

| Local stage | Evidence | Result owner |
|---|---|---|
| Establish method | Available teacher, text, memory, or experiment; compatible resources | Cultivation records chosen method and conditions |
| Practice | Deliberate exercise with current capacity and useful feedback | Resolution determines attempt and costs |
| Understand | Observed relation between technique, body, power, or environment | Cultivation records demonstrated insight |
| Integrate | Repeatable application under a meaningful new condition | Progression evaluates a changed capability |
| Break through | Named prerequisite satisfied and transition attempted | Progression commits the earned change once |


Leveling increases capacity; cultivation changes control and form. One activity may justify both, but they require distinct evidence and effect identities. Training opportunity is not automatic instant mastery unless an accepted power actually grants it. Preserve exceptional abilities rather than inventing universal training caps to suppress them.

## Technique creation

Source: SKILLPROG.SYSTEM, SPONTANEOUS.AWAKENING, HIDDENCLASS.TRIGGER, CLASSGEN.DYNAMIC, CLASSEVOL.RULE. Use the full [progression helper](../isekai-nexus-character-and-progression/SKILL.md#d-references-progression-rules-md) for actual awards and conditions.

Track desired effect, existing abilities, mechanism, attempted execution, observations, working scope, and the next useful experiment. Creative use receives a fitting development rather than a rote refusal. Separate proof of a useful trick from a completed high-tier ability. A technique's name cannot substitute for a defined effect and usable capacity.

Branch, Fuse, Mutate, and Ascend are routes from demonstrated use. A creative hybrid shares its component costs until a justified new rule is established; counting the same effect twice adds no power. Conceptual ascension requires the actual conceptual rules. Hidden classes respond to their source conditions, not every named invention.

## Personal systems

Use the accepted record as authority for trigger, promised benefit, costs, constraints, suspension, and recovery. Track eligibility, tasks actually offered, accepted tasks, evidence, fulfillment, and relationship response. Distinguish conditions the character can observe from deliberate mysteries. Check underlying eligibility consistently even when an interface is deceptive.

When a condition is met, provide the promised benefit. Guidance, a training opportunity, a reward, and a power each use their recorded mechanics. A conditional tutor can serve academy or cultivation practice; it adds no second automatic EXP event. End conditional access when its term ends without deleting unrelated earned skills. If the player declines an optional task, apply only a consequence actually contained in accepted terms. Personality cannot invent a new contract.

## Recovery and remembered futures

Track memory source, claimed past/future, current evidence, divergence, sealed ability, release condition, and recovered usable effect. A veteran can understand more than the present body can perform. Use remembered expertise to create useful approaches; preserve current resource and capacity conditions.

For each seal, establish a meaningful route: bodily development, compatible equipment, researched method, attunement, or a personal breakthrough. Fulfilled conditions release the named capability through progression once. Do not keep moving the goal after success. A time-travel start supplies knowledge from a particular history; actual divergence can invalidate forecasts. NPCs retain their own knowledge and reasons for belief.

Time resets require an accepted enabling ability and the actual SAVELOAD rules in progression-rules.md. Native Awakening does not move time backward. Campaign record maintenance does not grant an in-fiction rewind.

## Montage attachment data

Supply chosen learning objectives, methods, repeated exercises, practice limits, feedback sources, resource budgets, time horizon, current evidence, and stop events. Stop events include a breakthrough needing a choice, a new risky method, new patron terms, an important relationship decision, a release condition requiring action, or an unchosen assessment/expedition. Established low-stakes feedback can be compressed; substantive choices cannot.


World-specific disciplines, campus support, threat containment, and cultivation stages come from the full supplied world. Preserve their actual conditions rather than importing another destination's curriculum or power system.

Preserve character identity, possessions, earned skills, prior world history, and accepted system terms. Local compatibility can constrain access through documented conditions; it cannot erase earned development. A fresh destination start package is not granted to an existing traveler.

## d-references-montage-rules-md

# Montage agreement

Montage compresses presentation and advances declared activities through their existing procedures. It targets one loop or several cooperating loops. It has no independent EXP, reward, success, failure, cultivation, or relationship formula.

## Interval record

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Identity and incoming state | Interval identity, character/world revision, starting location/time, existing effects |
| Scope | Chosen objectives, participating loops, repeatable activities, desired horizon or stop condition |
| Delegation | Routine actions authorized, acceptable approaches, decisions reserved for the player |
| Budget | Available and permitted materials, funds, effort, travel, recovery, other actual commitments |
| Schedule | Sequential or concurrent activities, time basis, existing appointments/deadlines |
| Evidence | Distinct challenges, demonstrated changes, task/contract proofs, actual completed work |
| Checkpoints | Committed attempts/effects, elapsed time, remaining plan, earliest stop event |
| Return | Current scene and pending choice; completed, paused, revised, or abandoned interval |

Capture supplied facts and infer ordinary scheduling from the accepted plan. Ask only a material missing choice. “Study for a week” can reuse the current lesson and method; it does not authorize spending all savings or accepting a new pact. An unspecified horizon needs a bounded proposal or one question before skipping time. “Until I master this” permits progress toward the stated goal, not a promise that any goal is reached in an invented duration.

## Scheduling and causal order

Use established task durations and recipe yields. If none exists, establish a world-compatible duration/quantity for this plan and distinguish it from a source formula. Qualitative durations are valid. Resolve sequential activities from each preceding result; do not resolve a month's work against an unchanged opening record. Concurrent objectives can share an activity, but active time and resources cannot be used twice. Include actual recovery, attendance requirements, travel, supply access, and relevant obligations. Optional patron tasks remain optional.

Resolve representative meaningful attempts and genuine repeated batches. Group already-established routine work into a batch with its actual quantity, resource consumption, time, and evidence. Do not fabricate a successful challenge for every repeated motion or compute EXP as days multiplied by a guessed rate. Select observable milestones rather than narrating every exercise. Accepted accelerated or unlimited-benefit systems keep their actual promise; no new cap is imposed merely to limit compressed advancement.

Check established world events during elapsed time. NPCs and institutions continue according to their actual circumstances. No automatic invasion, relationship decay, catastrophe, Crown Lock failure, or tribulation arises merely because the player chose downtime. A genuinely scheduled event remains relevant. Preserve remembered-history claims and actual divergence.

## Effect and checkpoint agreement

Use [scene state](SKILL.md#d-references-scene-state-md) and [loop protocol](SKILL.md#d-references-loop-protocol-md). Resolution adjudicates all results and costs and commits health/MP/conditions/time. Economy commits inventory/funds. Progression commits earned advancement. Social and Domain commit their respective facts. Montage owns the interval, schedule, pacing, checkpoints, and return; it commits none of those effects a second time.

One batch has one attempt identity and separately owned effect identities. A garden practical can support academy, cultivation, and technique development through one outcome; it earns one EXP award plus any distinct justified control/technique effects. A verified recurring contract can pay for genuinely separate work; repeating its receipt cannot pay again. Domain income uses actual eligible periods, never one payment per narrated scene.

Apply milestones in order for every earned level. Leave unspent points and pending evolution choices intact unless the player already delegated a valid spending rule. Pause at a material evolution or power change needing acceptance. A passive increase with no reserved choice can occur during compression. Save checkpoints in available state before narration; durable writes require actual storage. If interrupted after partial application, retain applied identities and finish only pending owner handoffs. Do not rewind an already elapsed interval because narration was interrupted.

## Stop events

Stop at the earliest event requiring a choice outside the declared delegation:

- Budget, supplies, health, or resource reserve reaches its stated limit.
- A new bargain, binding commitment, risky method, material purchase, or important relationship decision appears.
- A breakthrough, class replacement, release condition, or Authority discovery needs the player's direction.
- A novel threat, uncertain route, meaningful mission encounter, disputed settlement, or changed future requires a new approach.
- A scheduled event changes what can reasonably continue.
- An unchosen world passage is ready to commit, or a new arrival scene begins. An explicitly delegated, valid crossing uses Travel and Arrival; the tailored introduction returns to full scene pacing.

Do not freeze routine work at every small variation. Honor explicitly delegated low-risk decisions and previously accepted methods. A declared hunt can compress established routine encounters within its agreed scope; a new materially different threat returns to a scene. Summarize only completed activity, state the actual elapsed period and progress in-world, then present the live choice. Preserve the unspent budget and unfinished plan.

## Render and resume

Use a few concrete moments tied to the character's attention, methods, relationships, and world. Show change through practiced ease, an improved design, a tested technique, useful information, or actual earned recognition. Include concise meaningful numbers when established and useful; keep internal ledgers and category labels silent. Do not claim success for a pending attempt or an accepted relationship for an unasked NPC.

Resume the remaining interval from its checkpoint after the player resolves the stop event or changes the plan. A new duration extends the plan from current time; it does not rerun the completed interval. Status and administrative questions consume no fictional time. Revoking compression returns to the current full scene with actual progress preserved.

Montage applies the shared mechanics to the player-approved plan.
