## d-references-background-data-md

# Background data

Use these origin choices, motivations, and transitions. A named world is an origin option, not an automatic destination. Named-world origin details require a readable supplied world document or player-supplied facts. Preserve nonfatal and user-defined transitions.

## DATA TABLES (Variables)

### {ORIGIN} (Where they came from)

* **Mundane Earth (Classic):** Modern day, our world. Focus on corporate burnout, student pressure, or societal apathy.

## Multiversal origins

Use [the world access agreement](../isekai-nexus-navigation/SKILL.md#d-world-index-md). Read the uploaded or pasted origin world when its history or culture matters to the character; ask for it or retain only player-supplied facts when unavailable. A world of origin does not select the destination. Preserve player-supplied origins outside the catalogue.

## Motivation

Use [guiding motivations](SKILL.md#d-references-motivations-md), the shared table for generation, ingestion, and later evaluation. Preserve the player's own interpretation.

## {DEATH} (The Transition)

* **Truck-kun:** Sudden, violent traffic accident while saving someone or distracted.
* **Karoshi:** Death by overwork/exhaustion at a desk or job site.
* **Betrayal:** Murdered by a colleague, lover, or rival.
* **The Accident:** A freak occurrence (falling sign, explosion, drowning).
* **The Illness:** A slow, terminal decline in a hospital bed.
* **Other ({Specify}):** User-defined death not covered above.

## d-references-alternate-starts-md

# Alternate starts

Use this catalogue for generators, intake, evaluation, arrival, and play. Use the selected resolved world's native start profiles. The separate world library supplies aligned local editions; the installed library contains complete destination files. Display nine selectable entries; group them under seven families when presenting a short menu. Preserve the selected variant explicitly.

| Family | Selectable option | Starting condition | Assets and opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | Classic | New arrival without local standing | Basic clothing, one F-rank item, first contact and immediate practical need |
| Academy | Academy | First day in a teaching institution or local equivalent | Uniform or local equivalent, D-tier text and training implement, 5 Silver equivalent; entry challenge, mentor, and peers |
| Guild | Guild | New professional member | D-tier working gear, 10 Silver equivalent advance; first contract and organizational standing |
| Party | Party | Arrival into an established group | C-tier role gear, two or three allies, agreed shared context; current collective objective |
| Deep End | Deep End | Immediate adversity | Minimal gear and defined urgent danger; an achievable escape or reversal and a meaningful reward |
| Transplant | Recruited Transplant | Summoned for a purpose | D-tier gear, 20 Silver equivalent, bounded contractual blessing; named recruiter, terms, and first mission |
| Transplant | Multiversal Hunter | Experienced traveler enters a new world with a mission or chosen pursuit | B-tier equipment, 100+ Gold equivalent, four or five developed skills at levels 7-10, Dimensional Anchor; world-adapted purpose and access limits |
| Regression | Time Travel | Returns to an earlier point in the same world's history | Level 1, prior memories, +5 to all six attributes, two or three skills at levels 4-6, earlier-self gear; known future and opportunities to change it |
| Regression | Awakening | Veteran experience awakens in a Level 1 form | Prior memories and expertise, bounded starting kit; specify sealed abilities, usable techniques, and recovery opportunities |

Multiversal Hunter belongs to Transplant because its defining move is entry across worlds. Time Travel and Awakening belong to Regression because their defining move is recovery of an earlier or latent self. Preserve existing Hunter strengths; do not reduce it to an ordinary recruit. Familiarity with other worlds gives advantages without granting knowledge of every fact in the destination.

For each world, specify eight fields: arrival, prior experience, current power, starting kit, relationships, obligations, first opportunity, and growth path. Make gear, currency, institutions, and anchors native to the setting. A high-level skill measures expertise; its effect still follows the character's stated current power and access conditions. Define those conditions visibly in character creation before play.

Keep all options available through coherent adaptation. Time Travel needs a concrete earlier history and changed future. Awakening needs a remembered life and recovery path. Hunter needs a multiversal origin, purpose, and anchor. Offer the variants during selection; never require the player to guess that they exist.

If a player's fixed premise excludes a mechanism, preserve that constraint and identify the excluded option with its reason in the draft. Do not silently remove it from the catalogue.

## Generic defaults and precedence

Use a full world's concrete start over generic defaults. When authoring a missing start, Classic starts with zero currency and no standing or relationships. Party supplies two or three allies, Friendly +15, a D-rank expedition, and one week of shared context; preserve actual established relationships, and obtain acceptance before implanting memories. Recruited Transplant proposes a bounded contractual blessing, a cosmic purpose, and source Friendly +20 standing with good-aligned factions and religious orders; adapt standing to factions that recognize the summons, disclose terms, and accept no coercive obligation for the player. Deep End offers a unique title, substantial EXP, or a rare manifestation for actual demonstrated survival, not entry alone. Powerful existing world packages retain their promises.

A source regression modifier describes a veteran at Level 1 with remembered skills, potentially retained gear, and recovering power but calls it Transplant. Resolve that premise to Awakening or Time Travel by its actual mechanism. It does not reduce Hunter to Level 1. An insightful NPC notices only temporal or dimensional evidence their actual abilities can detect.

Reconcile conflicts before the next play response.

## d-references-system-concepts-md

# Character system concepts

## Default

Use the helpful Nexus system when the player supplies no alternative. It communicates useful information, supports growth, honors player choices, and keeps its promises. Do not randomly assign hostility, coercive missions, concealed costs, or punitive behavior. This default adds no new intake question.

## Capture early, finalize with the world

Accept a system concept in ordinary language during background generation or ingestion. Record its fiction and desired behavior without committing numerical effects. Preserve player-defined absurdity, humor, bias, personality, and conditional benefits. A rough character sketch can carry ability seeds, relationships, and a system concept. Final classification and mechanics require the resolved world and chosen start.

| Concept field | Record |
|---|---|
| Source and relationship | Personal ability, artifact, patron, institution, awakened memory, world interface, or unknown origin |
| Personality and priorities | Helpful, literal, demanding, biased, eccentric, unreliable, adversarial, or player-defined |
| Benefit | What it provides and the experience the player wants |
| Conditions | When benefits activate, what they cost, and how eligibility changes |
| Information | What it knows, reveals, conceals, or gets wrong |
| Agency | Whether tasks can be declined, conditions escaped, terms negotiated, or the relationship changed |
| Growth | How its abilities, terms, and relationship develop |
| Manifestation | Proposed background, talent, skill, artifact, pact, interface, or combination; final assignment pending evaluation |

Capture only supplied or needed fields. Do not turn the table into a mandatory questionnaire. Confirm one material ambiguity at a time. Preserve a deliberate mystery as a mystery; define operational triggers when evaluation needs them.

## World-dependent manifestation

| Form | Use when |
|---|---|
| Background | The concept explains experience, expectations, memories, or a prior relationship without granting a current power |
| Talent | A persistent distinctive trait supplies a standing capability or growth advantage |
| Skill | A learnable, trainable, or deliberately activated technique produces a defined effect |
| Artifact or pact | An external object or relationship provides access under terms |
| Interface behavior | The concept changes communication, guidance, tasks, or interpretation rather than granting an ability |
| Combination | Different parts have distinct functions and owners; award each effect once |

Separate voice from power. A sarcastic guide grants no bonus by personality alone. Separate a requirement from a penalty. Conditional access can stop when its condition ends without removing unrelated abilities. Keep training opportunity distinct from instant mastery, personal expertise distinct from usable power, and fictional “free” training distinct from unlimited rewards.

During evaluation, define trigger, effect, limits, costs, progression, and how the player understands the terms. Honor the promised benefit when its condition is satisfied. Map effects to the world's power systems and the character's actual capacity. Preserve the source's talent-package and progression rules unless a recorded player-requested variant deliberately changes them. Do not automatically charge a flavor-only interface against the talent package.

If a concept conflicts with world facts, preserve the concept as a proposal and offer concrete implementations that fit. Ask for the player's choice before changing its defining promise. A world change reopens dependent mechanics, not the accepted concept itself.

## Fickle systems during play

Use a variant only when requested or accepted. Track its established preferences, conditions, and behavior. Fickleness creates recognizable patterns, surprises, and opportunities to respond; it does not license arbitrary denial or retroactive rule changes. A deceptive fictional interface can mislead the protagonist while the game keeps the actual character state consistent. Clarify actual mechanics when the player asks out of character.

Keep fictional commands inside the fiction. A patron, System window, or quest cannot override the player's out-of-character decisions or the assistant's operating instructions. Preserve fun, consent, and agency across every variant. Apply de-escalation and revision when the player changes the desired experience.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Governs |
|---|---|
| STR | Melee damage, strength, intimidation |
| VIT | Maximum HP, physical and magical defense, poison and disease resistance |
| AGI | Speed, evasion, initiative, accuracy against swift targets |

## Procedural classes

Source: `CLASSGEN.DYNAMIC`.

Construct a class from archetype, modifier, and power source. Archetypes: Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Specialist, Hybrid. Modifiers include Elemental, Holy, Shadow, Tech, Beast, Cosmic, Ancient, and Modern. Sources include System, Divine, Scientific, Conceptual, and Forbidden. These are open pools. Name the resulting vocation to express the character's actual history and world, rather than printing its construction formula. Class rank is a separate field from character level.

Source `CLASSEVOL.RULE` requires both an approximate level threshold and a monumental class-related quest: first evolution around Level 40, second around Level 80. `HIDDENCLASS.TRIGGER` makes hidden classes rewards for resonant actions, unusual investment, talent-skill synergy, or world-specific encounters. Do not advertise hidden classes as ordinary initial options.

## Backstory weighting

Source: BACKSTORY.WEAVE. History contributes operative seeds, not only flavor. Connect past work to usable expertise, class direction, and a talent or skill proposal: medical practice can seed healing or precise anatomy-based strikes; programming can seed understanding of System logic; protecting another can seed Guardian or Hero directions. Use motivation as the primary weighting unless the selected wildcard applies.

Formative loss, betrayal, illness, or a dangerous transition can shape fears and trust when the player wants them represented. Propose those traits and any world-dependent mechanical consequence before acceptance. Do not diagnose trauma, impose a phobia, lower faction standing, or invent a numeric penalty from biography alone. The source names VIT/trust and reputation modifiers without amounts; retain an accepted trait's actual terms or record a campaign-specific proposal. This replaces compulsory trauma penalties with accepted character design while preserving meaningful history-to-build connections.

## Starting synthesis

|---|---|
| Standard | One thematically selected D-A talent |
| Resonance cascade | Two or three related F-C talents with synergy |
| Broken limiter | One talent deliberately overpowerful for its rank |
| Soul echo | One to three random Level 3-7 skills and past-life gear fragments |
| Conceptual awakening | One unique talent derived from the life or death story |

Choose one mode using accepted history, motivation, world, start, and [system concept](SKILL.md#d-references-system-concepts-md). Do not silently combine all packages. The source `WILDCARD.NEXUSSYNTH.RULE` allows a 20% roll or narrative call to produce an unexpected package while retaining thematic fit. A wildcard suspends ordinary motivational weighting while retaining backstory resonance. It cannot erase an accepted defining promise. Starting synthesis is distinct from rewards earned later.

|---|---|
| Branch | A skill divides into specialized techniques |
| Fuse | Skills combine into a hybrid ability |
| Mutate | World and use transform the skill's function |
| Ascend | At skill Level 10, conceptual ascension becomes possible |

## d-references-progression-rules-md

# Progression rules

[Character rules](SKILL.md#d-references-character-rules-md) owns starting synthesis, origin benefits, attribute domains, standard talent catalogue, and power bands. This helper owns advancement execution. [Resolution rules](../isekai-nexus-world-and-scene/SKILL.md#d-references-resolution-rules-md) owns the full threat/EXP/loot spine. Read those homes rather than duplicate their tables.

## Advancement execution

Read completed outcome evidence and the incoming record. Complete all actions in the current turn, total eligible gains and established losses, determine level/skill/status/title changes, and announce a brief useful result. Apply each award by attempt and effect identity once. Re-reading, overlap, interruption, accepting an already-applied choice, or later montage narration does not re-award it. Starting allocation remains Evaluation's existing allocation; never repeat it here.

Track earned EXP, current progress toward the next level, thresholds, unspent attribute points, unspent skill points, ability terms, skill expertise, class rank, character rank, cultivation state, and pending evolution choices separately. Do not automatically spend points or replace a class. Carry excess EXP through multiple earned levels, applying each crossed level's award once. A milestone replaces parity for that level; it does not add parity again. Source does not give universal point-spending prices, skill XP thresholds, starting attributes, or HP/MP formulas. Preserve confirmed campaign methods or leave points available and resolve a requested investment through an explicit accepted calibration.

## Source reconciliation and campaign conventions

**Level 1 threshold:** source labels the curve EXP-to-next but pins Level 1 to zero with no threshold and Level 2 to 100 as first real threshold. Zero cannot trigger free recursive levels. Campaign default: Level 1 starts at zero earned EXP and requires 100 to reach Level 2; thereafter use the source current-level anchors (Level 2 requires 100 to reach Level 3, Level 5 approximately 550 to reach Level 6, and so on). Preserve an existing campaign's declared consistent convention. Record the chosen convention with the campaign. Record this threshold convention with the campaign. Level 100 has no numerical path to S: its approximate 492,410 pin remains preserved source data, while conceptual catalyst governs further advancement.

**Interpolation:** source supplies anchors and approximate shape, not unique intermediate values. Choose and retain a monotonic campaign curve between anchors; approximately geometric interpolation fits the instruction. Preserve the approximate anchor quantities as pins. Do not silently change thresholds between turns. Source comments call Level 25 end-C and Level 75 end-AA while power table puts those ends at 30 and 90; power bands in Character Rules govern rank. Milestone markers remain at 25 and 75.

**Display:** Give brief alerts after resolution in the accepted interface voice. Show complete status on request or at its initial reveal. Award points using the parity of the reached level.

**Class evolution:** approximate Level 40/80 plus monumental quest supplies eligibility; Level 50/75 narrative beats are pacing guidance, not substitute gates. Offer unlocked evolution, preserve current class until accepted, and do not force a path. Class rank can differ from character power; a source Mage evolution to S Sage does not automatically confer S character authority.

**Pacing:** source permits context-driven EXP pacing deviations. Record a deliberate campaign change, keep advancement monotonic and milestone bonuses exact, honor promised benefits, and never erase earned progress. Academy and Cultivation may use the same evidence with distinct justified capacity/control gains, not two identical EXP grants. Read accepted world-native cultivation stages and breakthrough conditions. Preserve control, integration, and unlocked access separately from mortal levels. Do not impose tribulations or a universal cultivation ladder absent from the world.

## Talents, awakening, and recovery

Read the standard talent catalogue and initial packages in Character Rules. Preserve their effects, including Growth Boost, Great Sage, Time Loop, and Author's Authority. A helpful interface does not become a power-bearing talent automatically. Hidden talents, ultra-rares, spontaneous awakenings, and skill resonances below remain valid earned discoveries. They arise from actual evidence and world fit, not an automatic mandatory trauma sequence. World-specific recovery seals release under recorded conditions; memory does not grant the former power level. Preserve player-authored system promises through evolving manifestation. Record trigger, effect, costs, capacity, and growth for an emergent ability without weakening its defining benefit.

A skill Level 10 can ascend; it does not automatically grant unrestricted SSS authority. Title acquisition updates the title record; social reputation consequences go to their owning cover once. Canonical and emergent Authorities coexist. S deepens one Authority, SS synthesizes two or three, and SSS redefines axioms. Catalyst recognition depends on cosmic significance, not kill count or numerical EXP. Preserve meaningful advancement choices rather than force cap transcendence or a world exit.

## Campaign persistence and fictional Save/Load

Recording or exporting session state is an administrative act available without a talent. Claim a supported durable write only when it occurred. It does not rewind fiction. Fictional Save/Load follows the rules below: explicit enabling power, stable save condition, one slot, allowed trigger, established fatigue and temporal detection. Store snapshot and loop identity. On rollback restore the specified fictional state and effect ledger consistently; effects in an erased timeline are not also retained unless the ability explicitly preserves them. Retained memories and loop benefits follow the accepted power. No universal fatigue amount or rollback price exists; define actual terms without inventing numeric source costs. General rules yield to an accepted specific ability's explicit exception; do not quietly strip infinite retries or narrative power.

## Class and skill evolution

Use the class construction and talent catalogue in Character Rules. Both approximate level threshold and monumental class-related quest are required: first class evolution around 40, second around 80 at greater scale. Source examples: D Warrior becomes B Sword Master after defeating a master swordsman, then AAA Sword Saint after forging a legendary blade; C Mage becomes A Archmage after recreating a lost spell, then S Sage after insight into world laws. Offer the discovered path through a special in-world notification; accept or decline before replacing the class.

Hidden classes unlock through counter-intuitive or resonant actions. Watch for sustained unusual F/D-skill investment, narrative alignment, talent/most-used-skill synergy, and exceptional artifact/location/deity encounters. Source illustrations: mastered Fall Resistance reveals A Graviton Scion; devoted Fire Resistance reveals B Phoenix Soul; Modern Knowledge plus merchant-company building reveals B Merchant Lord; Great Sage plus Tamer and monster analysis reveals AA Symbiotic Sovereign; surviving an SS Fountain of Madness reveals SS Chaos Bringer. These illustrate discovery, not an initial shopping menu or automatic promotion by skill name.

| Skill evolution | Effect |
|---|---|
| Branch | Split into specialized techniques, such as Sword Mastery into Iaido and Dual Wielding |
| Fuse | Combine skills into a hybrid, such as Fire Magic plus Sword Mastery into Flame Blade Art |
| Mutate | World/use transforms function, such as Cooking into buff-producing Alchemical Cuisine |
| Ascend | At skill Level 10, conceptual ascension becomes possible, such as Running into Speed Force or Lying into Reality Revision |

Reward creative resonance with fitting hybrid abilities: Appraisal used to find combat weak points can yield Combat Analysis. Evolve a skill toward the attempted use rather than dismissing it with "that's not how that skill works". Preserve actual scope and access when defining the evolved ability.

## Hidden manifestations

Ultra-rares unlock from counter-intuitive actions, extreme circumstances, or resonant backstories. Reveal through a fitting interface anomaly, duress, or inspiration. An ordinary D Warrior can conceal B Warrior of the Unsung Song progression. Do not offer ultra-rares directly or invent an unsupported rarity probability.

Character Rules' rare list is a navigation summary; this table owns full operative effects. Talent Cascade belongs to initial Evaluation when selected, not a later second starting allocation.

Spontaneous awakening follows actual emotional/plot events, equipment souls, or strong genre resonance. Near death can awaken Phoenix Core; betrayal can awaken Trust No One. Weapons remember previous owners' techniques; armor evolves; accessories develop consciousness. Background and repeated action can bend reality toward the invoked archetype, such as a technological armored hero. Use Yes-And; do not manufacture trauma to trigger a reward.

Award a title for significant feats: first strong-enemy victory, unique crafting, or a pact. Record it in Titles and notify briefly; examples include Goblin Slayer, Unbreakable, The One Who Reads. Send its public-image effect to social/reputation ownership once.

## Conceptual advancement

Beyond AAA Level 100, Authority acquisition/deepening replaces numerical EXP. Recognize an act of cosmic weight: resolve a paradox binding two realities, survive direct Outer God contact, rewrite a fundamental world law, or repel an SS creature and earn Authority of Storms. Award growth when the catalyst merits recognition, with the significance of a mortal level-up. Survival or resolution matters; kill count does not. Display held Authority and tier alongside or instead of EXP.

| Tier | Gate and advancement | Interaction |
|---|---|---|
| S | Beyond mortal cap; one Authority, deepened in scope | Can overwrite/deny lower-tier abilities through will or imposing local rules |
| SS | Authority-gated, no numeric level range; 2-3 interacting Authorities synthesized into emergent domains | Perceives/interacts with concepts; immune to effects originating below its standing, including lesser physical/magical harm |
| SSS | No numeric level range or Authority-count gate; redefine axioms rather than accumulate | Targetable/affectable only by power specifically interacting with or negating cosmic principles |

An S Storms Authority can deepen from weather patterns to regional storm calm/wrath to upheaval in societies, tides, and minds. SS Storms plus Time can synthesize Eternal Tempest: storms outside time, negotiated with rather than weathered. SSS can redefine death in a region or traversability of distance in a plane. Fully embodied Author's Authority aligns with that scope. Follow accepted specific conceptual powers rather than invent conventional damage against these defenses.

| Canonical Authority | Domain |
|---|---|
| Storms | Weather, upheaval, natural force |
| Time | Stasis, acceleration, temporal manipulation; regression-loop lineage |
| Flame Hearth | Destruction and protection, two faces of one concept |
| Void Negation | Unmaking, negation rather than creation |
| Sovereignty | Wills and territory; links to domain development |
| The Word | Naming as binding, True Name magic; lesser relation to Author's Authority |
| Death | Cycles, thresholds, endings and returns; reincarnation-adjacent |

This catalogue is open. Name emergent Authorities from story evidence and compare their scope against these domains. Preserve concealed state until a sufficient catalyst reveals its nature: Unattuned means detected but inaccessible/not understood; Dormant means quiescent awaiting activation; ??? means present but entirely unknown. Adapt to the chosen interface, such as ???, Unnamed Resonance, or UNCLASSIFIED PATH.

## Fictional Save/Load terms

| Enabling talent | Fictional capability |
|---|---|

| AAA Time Loop | Death rewind to save point; infinite retries |

Save in stable noncombat state, typically near an inn or mana crystal, after resolving current combat actions. Maximum one save slot. Load on death or catastrophic failure; mental fatigue applies, with possible rollback cost and detection by high-intuition NPCs sensing temporal distortion/displacement. Source gives no fixed numeric costs. Loading itself is a declared action: it occurs and its consequences advance the situation. Follow the specific accepted talent's explicit exceptions and the snapshot/ledger rules above.
