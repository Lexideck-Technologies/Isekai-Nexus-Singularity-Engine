# Multiversal travel

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus/references/loop-protocol.md), [adventure records](../isekai-nexus/references/adventure-loops.md), [character record](../isekai-nexus/references/character-record.md), [arrival state](../isekai-nexus/references/arrival-state.md), and complete origin world. Use the common silent output frame.

Activate for preparing an interworld passage, choosing a destination, returning through an anchor, or exercising an accepted conceptual authority. Local journeys belong to Exploration. Own the transfer episode, addresses, anchors, and continuity references; shared owners retain mechanical effects.

1. Preserve the continuing character, earned abilities, possessions, companions, history, and accepted personal system. Record intended destination and transit method, actual anchor/address, energy and permission requirements, and return expectations. A Hunter label supplies no unrecorded free portal.
2. Resolve a complete destination through [World Resolution](../isekai-world-resolution/SKILL.md). Prefer the established campaign instance when revisiting. If the destination world is not readable, check the local world library, then request its complete document if still unavailable and stop before passage, preserving origin and pending travel. Do not reconstruct an unavailable authored destination. An explicitly requested original destination must be completed and selected first.
3. Read the destination's actual access conditions. Use [Character Evaluation](../isekai-character-evaluation/SKILL.md) to evaluate only dependent local compatibility, preserving earned capacity and existing allocation. Explain a defining proposed adaptation and obtain acceptance before commitment. Do not grant the destination's new-character kit, origin bonuses, or default Level 1 state.
4. Resolve the chosen transit through [Resolution](../isekai-resolution/SKILL.md) with accepted method and capacity. Spend energy once and preserve actual passenger/item scope. Use the supplied worlds' actual address, anchor, energy, and negotiated-access requirements; a coordinate alone never grants unrecorded transit. Local attunement limits documented access without erasing expertise.
5. After committed crossing and accepted local access, create a new entry episode for the same character through [Arrival](../isekai-arrival/SKILL.md). Stop at its first playable decision. An interrupted crossing resumes its recorded stage without a second spend, second kit, or duplicate entry.
6. For conceptual action consult full [progression rules](../isekai-nexus/references/progression-rules.md) and source CONCEPTUAL.LEVELING, CATALYST, and relevant tier/authority rules. Use only accepted authority at earned scope; travel or high skill expertise never automatically ascends the character. Route an actual Save/Load declaration through its enabling talent and SAVELOAD.RULE. Recording campaign continuity is separate from fictional rewind.

Expose repeatable route research, anchor maintenance, or transit preparation, evidence, time/cost basis, and stop events for Montage. Destination selection, defining access choices, actual passage commitment, and arrival are decision boundaries. Montage cannot silently create a world, cross into it, or confer conceptual power.

Read the [Codex play contract](../isekai-nexus/references/codex-play-contract.md) when entering play or restoring a campaign after context loss.
