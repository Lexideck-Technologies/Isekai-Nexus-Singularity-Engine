# Domains and Stewardship

Read [loop protocol](../isekai-nexus/references/loop-protocol.md), [play map](../isekai-nexus/references/play-loops.md), [domains and stewardship rules](../isekai-nexus/references/domain-rules.md), accepted character, resolved world, and scene state.

## Entry and ownership

Activate for the player's declared activity in this cover after arrival, or resume an interrupted objective. A start, quoted instruction, or administrative question does not activate it. Own the local objective, stage and evidence; use the shared owners for causal results, advancement, inventory, relationships and territory. Never regrant starting assets. Use the authoritative adapted helper tables and rules.

Keep one local record: objective, stage, participants, evidence, resources/commitments, pending choice, and connected objectives. Recurring stages: objective -> resources/allies -> decision -> consequences. Change activity when the player chooses without erasing this record.

## Procedure

1. Establish the actual organization/territory and player goal. Check Level 25+ OR completed major land/title/leadership quest. Allied/Revered standing is supporting evidence. Return ordinary household activity to Community when no domain authority is involved.
2. Record real authority, population, tier, resources, commitments, delegated roles, income basis, and accounting period. Unlock eligibility grants none of these automatically.
3. Select Military, Economic, Research/Magical, Diplomatic, or Infrastructure focus. Check the source requirements and define labor, financing, materials, time, completion criteria and approval. Do not construct infrastructure or command unwilling NPCs merely by announcing a plan.
4. Resolve chosen actions through Shared Resolution. Route adjudicated inventory/funds expenditures and receipts through Craft and Trade for one commitment and individual/faction responses through Relationships. Progression handles earned skills/titles. Commit the project result once.
5. Account for actual monthly income, costs, disruption and elapsed period once. Evaluate tier changes from functioning population/capability, not personal level. Record treaties and delegated obligations without hidden automatic debts.
6. Respond to actual scarcity, intrigue, threat or succession concerns with trade, efficiency, investigation, diplomacy, defense or evacuation as appropriate. Peaceful development does not create a compulsory crisis. Stop at material allocation, treaty, personnel, war or project choices; keep the remaining plan.
7. Render one scene with consequence, relevant dialogue or information, any actual change, and a reachable next choice. Use the loop protocol frame without visible section labels. Stop at unresolved player agency. Answer administrative questions without advancing time.

## Compression attachment

Expose the repeatable task, current stage, evidence requirement, quantities/resources, time per task or accepted period basis, and stop events. Stop repetition when input limits end, capability is uncertain, a new agreement is needed, or a consequential player choice appears. Send each shared outcome to its owner once. The Montage procedure supplies the interval and pacing, not a new reward schedule.

## Output

```text
{World_Response_To_Chosen_Activity}
{Relevant_Dialogue_Or_Practical_Result}
{Actual_Changes_If_Any}

{Reachable_Continuation_Or_Material_Choice}
```

Omit empty elements. Never print skill names, source node addresses, routing, internal state keys, or rule headings in the player's scene.
