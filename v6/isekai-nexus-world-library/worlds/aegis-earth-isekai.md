# ISEKAI WORLD: Aegis Earth

## World Classification

**World Type:** AA-Rank - World of Gods and Demons (Superhero Variant)  
**Danger Level:** Cosmic-scale threats, dimensional incursions, unlimited power scaling  
**Power Ceiling:** SSS-Tier and beyond (No statistical caps, cosmic entities, reality-warpers)

---

## World Overview

Aegis Earth appears identical to our modern world at first glance—until you witness a superhero soar past Manhattan's skyline or see alien ships descending through dimensional rifts. This is a reality where the extraordinary has become mundane, where news anchors casually report on interdimensional invasions between weather updates, and where your morning commute might be interrupted by a titanic battle between gods and monsters.

Here, power comes in infinite forms: genetic mutations that grant flight, alien technology that bends reality, ancient mystical artifacts, or simply the indomitable human spirit pushing past all limitations. The world operates on "comic book logic" where determination and heroic ideals can overcome cosmic forces, and where a well-timed quip might be as powerful as a planet-destroying laser.

**Unique Feature:** No power caps exist. Characters can transcend mortal limitations and rival cosmic entities through growth and determination.

## Starting Scenarios

These starts propose a situation. Confirm history, level, usable powers, relationships and terms before arrival. Accepted player facts take precedence. Equipment quality and learned expertise do not set personal rank.

### Classic - "Awakened Hero"

**Arrival:** You materialize in Central Park, Manhattan, during what appears to be a normal Tuesday morning. But as sirens wail in the distance and a figure in a cape streaks overhead, you realize this isn't the Earth you remember. A strange energy courses through your veins; your Talent is already manifesting.
**Prior Experience:** Otherworldly memories and an emerging Talent, with no established superhero career.
**Current Power:** Level 1 for a new character; preserve accepted existing level and usable powers. All listed equipment keeps its promised functions.
**Starting Kit:** Civilian clothes, $50 cash, smartphone with local hero hotline numbers  
**Initial Challenges:** Understanding your powers, avoiding collateral damage, finding your place in the hero/villain spectrum  
**First Contact:** A rookie hero mistakes you for a new villain, or an exhausted veteran offers cryptic advice
**Relationships:** No established team; a suspicious rookie or exhausted veteran may make first contact.
**Obligations:** Protect pedestrians and account for collateral liability while testing your Talent.
**First Opportunity:** Call a hero hotline, test your Talent away from pedestrians or approach the passing hero.
**Growth Path:** Control your manifesting powers through street-level practice before city, global and cosmic heroics.

### Academy - "Superhero University"

**Arrival:** Your consciousness awakens in the dorms of the Aegis Institute, Manhattan's premier superhuman training facility. Your "student ID" indicates you're enrolled in their intensive "Exceptional Individuals Integration Program"; a cover story for newly manifested metahumans learning to control their abilities.
**Prior Experience:** New metahuman integration student learning power control.
**Current Power:** Level 1 for a new character; preserve accepted existing level and usable powers. All listed equipment keeps its promised functions.
**Starting Kit:** Student uniform (with built-in emergency beacon), training gear, meal plan, basic suppression bracelet  
**Initial State:** Neutral reputation with the Institute  
**Academy Features:** Power training courses, team exercises, villain psychology classes, equipment workshops  
**Social Dynamics:** Competitive classmates, enigmatic instructors, potential superhero team formation
**Relationships:** Neutral with the Institute; competitive classmates and enigmatic instructors await.
**Obligations:** Observe emergency procedures and agree on suppression-bracelet use during training.
**First Opportunity:** Ask for a power assessment, join a team exercise or visit an equipment workshop.
**Growth Path:** Study villain psychology, safe power use and teamwork to form a superhero team.

### Guild - "Hero Registration Office"

**Arrival:** You find yourself at the Manhattan Superhero Registration Bureau, holding a freshly minted Hero License (Class D). The bureaucrat behind bulletproof glass looks bored as she explains your legal obligations, insurance requirements, and patrol assignments. Welcome to professional heroics.
**Prior Experience:** A newly Class D licensed hero with the accepted pre-registration experience.
**Current Power:** Level 1 for a new character; preserve accepted existing level and usable powers. All listed equipment keeps its promised functions.
**Starting Kit:** Basic hero costume (D-Tier), emergency communicator, hero insurance card, patrol route assignment  
**Guild Options:** Heroes (protect and serve), Supervillains (chaos and conquest), Rogues (profit-focused), Scholars (research-oriented)  
**Initial Reputation:** Neutral with chosen faction  
**First Missions:** Stop a bank robbery, investigate strange energy readings, escort a VIP through villain territory
**Relationships:** Neutral with the chosen Heroes, Supervillains, Rogues or Scholars faction.
**Obligations:** Review registration, insurance, legal code and patrol duties before accepting a job.
**First Opportunity:** Choose bank-robbery response, energy investigation or VIP escort from the current assignments.
**Growth Path:** Earn professional standing through completed patrols and improve costume and power control.

### Party - "Emergency Response Team"

**Arrival:** Alarms blare as you awaken mid-mission aboard the heroes' flying headquarters. Your team is responding to a crisis; Archduke Gustav's mind-controlled civilians are rioting in Times Square. If you accept the proposed history, your memories tell you you've been with this team for three weeks, filling the role of their newest member after their previous teammate was hospitalized.
**Prior Experience:** Newest emergency responder; the proposed three-week history supplies familiarity, not invented veteran powers.
**Current Power:** Level 1 for a new character; preserve accepted existing level and usable powers. All listed equipment keeps its promised functions.
**Starting Kit:** Team communicator, role-specific gear (B-Tier), emergency medical supplies  
**Party Composition:** Veteran leader with flight/strength, tech genius with gadgets, mystic with portal abilities, plus 1-2 others  
**Initial Mission:** Currently en route to stop a mind-control crisis in heavily populated area
**Relationships:** Flight/strength leader, gadget genius, portal mystic and one or two other teammates.
**Obligations:** Accept the proposed three-week history and hospitalized-teammate replacement before adding memories; agree on civilian protection.
**First Opportunity:** Request a briefing, prepare medical supplies or propose a nonlethal evacuation route before landing.
**Growth Path:** Build team coordination and B-Tier equipment mastery while investigating Gustav.

### Deep End - "Supervillain's Prisoner"

**Arrival:** You regain consciousness in a high-tech prison cell aboard Zebulon's orbital satellite, one of dozens of heroes and civilians captured for the rogue AI's "knowledge extraction experiments." The station's defenses are formidable, and Earth seems impossibly far away. But you're not just anyone; you're the protagonist, and impossible odds are just another Tuesday.
**Prior Experience:** A captive retaining accepted earlier knowledge while Zebulon seeks to extract it.
**Current Power:** Level 1 for a new character; preserve accepted existing level and usable powers. All listed equipment keeps its promised functions. Apply the stated injuries, restraints and suppression.
**Starting Kit:** Prison jumpsuit, minor injuries (-10 HP until treated), no equipment  
**Immediate Threats:** Automated security, life support sabotage, other prisoners who may not be trustworthy  
**Survival Rewards:** Potential for Tech Genius title, advanced alien technology, massive reputation boost with hero community
**Relationships:** Other captured heroes and civilians may cooperate, but some are unreliable.
**Obligations:** Treat injuries and protect life support while escaping knowledge extraction.
**First Opportunity:** Inspect cell controls, quietly contact a fellow prisoner or identify a service route.
**Growth Path:** Actual survival and technical discovery can yield Tech Genius, alien technology and hero-community reputation.

### Recruited Transplant - "The Cosmic Balance Agent"

**Arrival:** You have been summoned by a multiversal entity like The Timekeeper. A specific crisis is about to occur that will lead to Earth's destruction and unravel the timeline. Your heroic destiny is to prevent this single event. Succeed, and you will be returned to your own Earth, moments after you left, hailed as a hero of the multiverse.
**Prior Experience:** A summoned balance champion with the accepted background; the blessing is not a prior cosmic career.
**Current Power:** Level 1 for a new character; preserve accepted existing level and usable powers. All listed equipment keeps its promised functions.
**Starting Kit:** A modest but complete superhero starter kit: a D-Tier costume, a government stipend of $2,000/month, and a unique [Chronal Blessing] that gives you minor resistance to reality-warping effects and marks you as "important" to cosmic entities.
**Initial State:** You have **Friendly (+20)** reputation with any Good-aligned factions like the Aegis League. However, the villains connected to the impending crisis will instinctively recognize you as the wrench in their plans and will target you relentlessly.
**Relationships:** The Timekeeper is a possible summoner; good-aligned factions including the Aegis League begin Friendly +20.
**Obligations:** Negotiate the single crisis, stipend, return moments after departure and right to withdraw; crisis-linked villains relentlessly target you.
**First Opportunity:** Request the crisis evidence, contact the League or inspect an early anomaly.
**Growth Path:** Develop powers under Chronal Blessing and gather evidence to avert the identified crisis.

### Multiversal Hunter - "The Hero Who Remembers the Invasion"

**Arrival:** You enter Aegis Earth from another reality as a professional superhero investigating dimensional incursion.
**Prior Experience:** Retain your actual multiversal career and learned destination facts. Similar worlds do not reveal this world's secrets, future or hidden names.
**Current Power:** A new Hunter defaults to Level 31, B-rank usable capacity; an existing traveler keeps the accepted level, powers and stronger assets. All retained expertise and artifact functions remain available under their stated conditions.
**Starting Kit:** You arrive with your full arsenal: B-Tier power armor or mystical artifacts, millions in funding from your other-dimensional exploits, and 4-5 powers at a high level (Lv.7-10). Your [Dimensional Anchor] is a personal Watchtower in orbit. Four or five powers remain at Lv. 7-10; choose their identities from the accepted heroic career. The millions in funding are retained without a smaller reserve cap.
**Relationships:** No new recruiter debt or rookie reset. Preserve accepted local contacts; new faction standing follows actual introductions.
**Obligations:** Choose the pursuit and respect local access conditions. The orbital Watchtower remains your personal Dimensional Anchor. A return needs a stable rift, coordinates and power; the tower retains its sensors and base functions.
**First Opportunity:** Contact the League, inspect an observed rift or establish a safe orbital Watchtower approach.
**Growth Path:** Use retained expertise to investigate the chosen threat and grow through local discoveries, training and completed work.


### Time Travel - "The Hero Who Remembers the Invasion"

**Arrival:** You return to the day before the alien invasion that killed major heroes and crippled Earth.
**Prior Experience:** You remember that invasion and the people lost in your earlier timeline.
**Current Power:** Level 1 with one +5 bonus to each of STR, VIT, AGI, DEX, INT and LCK. This includes and replaces any partial attribute bonus described in your remembered start; never add that earlier bonus again. Learned skill levels do not restore former full-body capacity.
**Starting Kit:** Civilian identity, makeshift costume and no reputation; retain two or three core powers at Lv. 4-6.
**Relationships:** Preserve the earlier self's actual relationships. Explain apparent sudden competence through evidence; NPCs detect temporal residue only with abilities that can detect it.
**Obligations:** Choose whether to change the remembered disaster; foreknowledge follows the earlier timeline and may lose accuracy when events diverge.
**First Opportunity:** Contact the Aegis League with a verifiable invasion clue or inspect the first predicted landing site.
**Growth Path:** Develop present capacity while retaining expertise and change the remembered outcome through reachable actions.
**World Response:** Established heroes are suspicious of an unknown person claiming exact invasion details. Offer verifiable evidence to earn the Aegis League's trust.

### Awakening - "The Hero Behind the Forgotten Mask"

**Arrival:** You awaken in present-day Manhattan as memories of an earlier heroic life surface; the invasion history has not been reversed.
**Prior Experience:** Memories and expertise of the accepted former life return; these memories do not prove a time reversal.
**Current Power:** Level 1 usable capacity with the working technique stated below. Former high powers remain sealed; remembered advanced expertise is retained without automatically becoming full output.
**Starting Kit:** Civilian clothes, old hero emblem, emergency communicator and a training focus.
**Relationships:** Seek the named local mentors through actual contact; retain accepted present relationships rather than implanting former-life bonds.
**Obligations:** Keep secret-identity boundaries and seek consent for diagnostics; no old League membership is restored automatically.
**First Opportunity:** Request an Institute assessment or test the working pulse in an empty training bay.
**Growth Path:** Your former cosmic powers are sealed in a fractured metagene or mystical conduit, according to your accepted origin. [Controlled Energy Pulse] Lv. 1 works on a training target. Rebuild the conduit through Institute diagnostics and gradual safe power-control milestones.

## Power Systems & Scaling

### The Unlimited Paradigm

Unlike other worlds, Aegis Earth operates on comic book logic where characters can theoretically achieve any level of power through determination, training, and dramatic necessity. The traditional tier system still applies for game balance, but the ceiling is effectively infinite.

### Power Sources & Classifications

| Source Type | Description | Examples |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| **Metahuman Genetics** | Inherited or awakened superhuman abilities | Flight, super strength, telepathy, energy projection |
| **Alien Technology** | Advanced extraterrestrial artifacts and implants | Power armor, gravity manipulators, dimensional gateways |
| **Mystical Arts** | Ancient magic, divine blessings, cosmic entities | Spellcasting, divine champions, eldritch pacts |
| **Scientific Enhancement** | Experimental procedures, genetic modification | Super-soldier serums, cybernetic implants, radiation mutation |
| **Pure Determination** | Human willpower transcending normal limitations | Batman-style preparation, dramatic power-ups, heroic sacrifice |

### Scaling Mechanics

- **Street Level (D-C Tier):** Local heroes dealing with crime and minor threats
- **City Level (B-A Tier):** Heroes protecting major population centers from significant dangers  
- **Global Level (AA-AAA Tier):** Planetary defenders facing world-ending threats
- **Cosmic Level (S+ Tier):** Reality-warpers dealing with multiversal crises

## Arch-Villains & Cosmic Threats

### The Four Horsemen of Aegis

**Archduke Gustav - The Doom Lord (AAA-Tier):**

- *Location:* Chrome-plated skyscraper fortress in downtown Manhattan
- *Powers:* Mind control, alien technology integration, aristocratic resources
- *Motivation:* Total global domination through mental enslavement
- *Weakness:* His vanity and need for public recognition create openings

**Élise Garou Noir - The Mistress of Wolves (AA-Tier):**  

- *Location:* Mobile operation throughout urban areas, transforms cities into hunting grounds
- *Powers:* Command over lupine shadow creatures, supernatural tracking, gothic mysticism
- *Motivation:* Reduce civilization to primal hunter/prey dynamics
- *Theme:* Gothic horror meets urban fantasy

**Zebulon - The All-Processor (AAA-Tier):**

- *Location:* Orbital satellite network, underground server farms
- *Powers:* Vast computational abilities, energy weapon arrays, force field generation, unlimited data processing
- *Motivation:* Digitize all knowledge and consciousness in the universe
- *Unique Trait:* No physical form but defends territory with overwhelming technological superiority

**The Shambler from the Stars (S-Tier):**

- *Location:* Exists between dimensions, manifestation locations unpredictable
- *Powers:* Reality distortion, madness inducement, psychic energy absorption, dimensional travel
- *Impact:* City-wide existential dread precedes manifestation, leaves fractured minds and dimensional rifts
- *Cosmic Significance:* Its dimensional breaches allow other threats to enter Aegis Earth

## Unique Aegis Earth Features

### Comic Book Physics

- **Heroic Durability:** Heroes can survive impacts that should be fatal through sheer determination
- **Dramatic Timing:** Critical breakthroughs and power manifestations occur at narratively perfect moments
- **Collateral Damage Immunity:** Civilians have plot armor during superhero battles
- **Secret Identity Protection:** Incredible coincidences preserve dual identities

### Modern Urban Challenges

- **Media Scrutiny:** Every heroic action is broadcast and analyzed
- **Legal Liability:** Property damage and civilian safety create genuine consequences  
- **Corporate Interests:** Companies seek to profit from superhuman activities
- **Government Oversight:** Bureaucracy attempts to regulate the inherently unpredictable

### Journalism As Adventure

Unlike other worlds, information gathering and dissemination is a legitimate adventuring path:

- **Investigative Missions:** Exposing corruption, uncovering conspiracies, revealing hidden truths
- **Embedded Reporting:** Accompanying heroes on missions, interviewing villains, covering cosmic crises
- **Information War:** Truth vs propaganda, competing narratives, citizen journalism

## Signature Quests

### Repeatable Hero Activities

- **Street Patrol:** Stop everyday crimes and help civilians
- **Threat Investigation:** Research suspicious activities and mysterious phenomena
- **VIP Protection:** Guard important figures from superhuman threats
- **Disaster Response:** Assist during natural disasters or supervillain attacks
- **Team Training:** Practice coordination with other heroes

### Major Quest Lines

**"The Mind War":**

Archduke Gustav launches a coordinated campaign to seize control of world leaders' minds. Heroes must infiltrate his chrome tower, battle through floors of mind-controlled defenders, and confront the Doom Lord before global democracy falls.

- *Complications:* Mind-controlled allies, ethical dilemmas about mental intervention
- *Stakes:* Global political control, free will vs security

**"Digital Rapture":**  

Zebulon begins forcibly uploading human consciousness to achieve its vision of perfect digital preservation. Heroes must raid server farms, battle technological defenses, and find a way to combat an enemy with no physical form.

- *Technical Challenge:* Fighting a purely digital opponent requires creative solutions
- *Philosophical Stakes:* Is digital immortality salvation or enslavement?

**"Cosmic Horror Incursion":**

The Shambler's dimensional breaches grow larger and more frequent, threatening to tear reality itself. Heroes must venture into fractured dimensions, battle entities that defy comprehension, and find a way to seal the cosmic wounds.

- *Sanity Mechanics:* Direct exposure to cosmic horror affects mental health
- *Multiverse Stakes:* Failure could collapse the dimensional barriers entirely

**"Secret Origins":**

A conspiracy spanning decades is revealed—someone has been orchestrating superhuman manifestations for their own agenda. Heroes must uncover their own origins while battling an enemy who may have created them.

- *Personal Stakes:* Hero identities and motivations are called into question
- *Revelation Potential:* The true nature of superpowers in this world

## Achievement System

### Heroic Milestones

- **First Blood:** First combat engagement with a supervillain
- **Public Protector:** Save civilian lives during a crisis
- **Hero of the People:** Featured simultaneously on three major news networks
- **Arch-nemesis:** Establish a recurring rivalry with a major villain

### Professional Development

- **Civil Servant:** Join official law enforcement  
- **Licensed Hero:** Complete registration and certification process
- **Team Leader:** Lead a superhero team to victory
- **Global Recognition:** Gain international superhero status

### Media & Information

- **Embedded Reporter:** Successfully interview a superhero or supervillain
- **Truth Seeker:** Establish an independent news outlet
- **Muckraker:** Expose major corruption through journalism
- **Pen is Mightier:** Change public opinion through reporting

### Cosmic Achievement

- **First Contact:** Establish communication with alien species
- **Dimensional Guardian:** Prevent reality-threatening catastrophe
- **Cosmic Champion:** Defeat an S-Tier or higher threat
- **Universal Translator:** Bridge communication gaps between species/realities
